# SATYUG (HOPE) — Complete App Description

## PART 1: APP IDENTITY AND PURPOSE

SATYUG is a consciousness development platform marketed under the product name HOPE, which stands for "A companion for becoming." The application is designed as an AI-powered personal companion that guides users through structured spiritual and psychological development practices. Unlike typical wellness apps that encourage ongoing engagement, SATYUG is explicitly designed to become unnecessary over time as users progress in their development — a business model the creators call "designed for obsolescence."

The platform bridges ancient wisdom traditions with modern artificial intelligence. It offers users a daily adaptive companion that assembles personalized practices, meditations, reflections, and conversations based on the user's current psychological state, patterns, energy level, and stage of development. The application is built around the concept of a "temple" — a sacred digital space where users go to practice, reflect, learn, and grow.

The core value proposition is that every session is unique and dynamically generated rather than pre-recorded. The AI analyzes the user's recent signals, detected loops, emotional patterns, and readiness to compose a bespoke experience for each interaction. This creates what the team calls a "living" app — one that evolves with the user rather than presenting static content.

## PART 2: CONSCIOUSNESS MODEL — THE INTELLECTUAL FOUNDATION

The entire application is built upon a proprietary consciousness model that serves as the competitive moat and differentiates SATYUG from every other wellness or meditation app on the market. This model is documented extensively in the architecture and IP documentation files within the project.

### The Seven Pillars of Practice

The application is organized around three core practices drawn from ancient yogic and contemplative traditions. These three pillars form the foundation of every session and program offered within the app. The first pillar is observation, also known as Sakshi Bhava or witness consciousness. This practice trains users to observe their thoughts, emotions, and sensations without identification or reaction. It is the foundational skill upon which all deeper practices depend. The second pillar is inquiry, specifically Atma Vichara or self-inquiry, where users investigate the nature of the self through the question "Who am I?" This practice systematically dismantles false identifications and leads toward recognition of one's true nature. The third pillar is release, drawing from the Neti Neti tradition of "not this, not this" negation, where users systematically release attachments, identifications, and patterns that no longer serve their development.

### V-Level System — Vertical Stages of Awakening

The consciousness model includes a vertical dimension called V-Level, consisting of seven distinct stages of awakening. These stages represent progressive levels of consciousness development, moving from initial recognition of inner conflict through to sustained awakened awareness. The seven stages are named and defined as follows:

The first stage is called The Crack, representing the initial breaking open of consciousness where the user first becomes aware that there is more to reality than their ordinary ego-based experience. The second stage is The Seeker, where active pursuit of understanding and practice begins. The third stage is The Explorer, where deeper investigation and experimentation with practices occurs. The fourth stage is The Contemplative, where sustained inner work and reflection become established habits. The fifth stage is The Witness, where the ability to observe without identification becomes stable and accessible. The sixth stage is The Liberated, where significant freedom from conditioned patterns has been achieved. The seventh and final stage is The Awakened, representing sustained, embodied awakened awareness.

Each V-Level is not simply a badge or rank but represents a qualitative shift in how the user experiences reality, processes challenges, relates to their patterns, and engages with practice. The application tracks progression through these levels using behavioral signals, session feedback, loop activity, and dimensional assessments.

### H-Level System — Horizontal Capacities

In addition to the vertical V-Level progression, the consciousness model includes twelve horizontal capacities that develop across the vertical stages. These capacities represent different dimensions of development that can grow independently of each other. The twelve H-Levels are Presence, Observation, Attention, Emotional Mastery, Self-Inquiry Depth, Release Capacity, Connection, Purpose Alignment, Embodiment, Resilience, Compassion, and Wisdom.

This creates a two-dimensional development matrix where users can be at different vertical stages while simultaneously developing varying levels of these twelve horizontal capacities. This allows for nuanced personalization — two users at the same V-Level may have completely different capacity profiles and thus require completely different practice recommendations.

### The Loop System — 400+ Psychological Patterns

One of the most data-intensive components of the consciousness model is the Loop System, which catalogs over 400 named psychological loops organized into seven primary categories. A loop is defined as a recurring psychological pattern that activates automatically in response to specific triggers, producing predictable emotional and behavioral outcomes. The seven categories of loops are:

Origin loops relate to questions of where one comes from, belonging, and foundational identity. Worth loops center on self-value, deservingness, and recognition. Safety loops involve security needs, threat detection, and protection strategies. Power loops concern control, agency, and influence over one's circumstances. Connection loops govern relationship needs, intimacy fears, and social bonding. Identity loops involve self-concept, role identification, and persona maintenance. Shadow loops represent the hidden, repressed, or disowned aspects of the psyche that operate unconsciously.

Each loop in the catalog has a defined trigger pattern, emotional signature, behavioral expression, root cause analysis, and mapped intervention strategies. The application actively monitors for loop activation through real-time signal detection and surfaces these patterns to users through mirror sessions, journal reflections, and adaptive content recommendations. Over time, users develop the capacity to recognize their own loops as they activate, which is considered a major milestone in consciousness development.

### Six Life Domains

Development is tracked across six distinct life domains to ensure balanced growth rather than lopsided spiritual advancement in one area while other areas remain unaddressed. The six domains are Inner Life, Relationships, Work and Purpose, Health and Energy, Creative Expression, and Contribution and Service. Each practice session, program, and recommendation is tagged to specific domains, and the application monitors domain balance over time to guide users toward holistic development.

### Dimensions of Experience

Beyond the structured V-Levels, H-Levels, and Loops, the consciousness model tracks over one hundred dimensions of human experience. These dimensions include subtle qualities of awareness, emotional granularity, sensory capacity, relational depth, and cognitive flexibility. The consciousness engine continuously updates dimension scores based on session outcomes, journal reflections, signal data, and practice engagement patterns. These dimension scores feed into the adaptive engine to refine session composition and guide selection in real time.

## PART 3: CORE FEATURES AND USER EXPERIENCE

### Daily Adaptive Themes

The application presents users with a unique daily theme that evolves as they progress through their journey. Each day's content is assembled dynamically based on the user's current state, recent loop activity, energy levels, emotional patterns, and developmental readiness. Two users at the same V-Level may receive completely different daily experiences because the adaptation engine considers their unique pattern signatures. This daily theming creates a sense of freshness and relevance that keeps users engaged while ensuring the content always meets them where they are.

### Practice Sessions

The heart of the application is the practice session experience. Sessions are not pre-recorded videos or static audio files. Instead, they are composed in real time by the Composer engine, which assembles experiences from atomic building blocks called ingredients. These ingredients include guided meditations, self-inquiry prompts, breathwork instructions, wisdom teachings, reflective questions, body awareness exercises, and silence periods.

When a user initiates a session, the system gathers a comprehensive context snapshot including their current V-Level and H-Level profile, active loops, recent session history, emotional state indicators, time of day, energy signals, and stated intentions. This context is sent to the Composer engine, which selects and sequences ingredients into a coherent session flow. The session is then delivered through the Session Shell interface, which manages the playback, timing, transitions, and real-time adaptations.

The session experience is highly immersive. It features a cinematic 3D environment with animated cosmic backgrounds, a breathing orb that visualizes the practice rhythm, ambient soundscapes, and a guide voice that delivers instructions and teachings. The guide voice is selected from a roster of thirty or more master voices, each representing a different spiritual tradition or teaching style. The guide matching algorithm considers the user's V-Level, preferred tradition, current practice needs, and recent feedback to select the most appropriate guide for each session.

### Guided Master Chat

Beyond structured sessions, users can engage in free-form conversations with AI guides who embody the voices and wisdom of various spiritual masters and teachers. The guide chat system uses a sophisticated prompt director that calibrates the guide's tone, depth, complexity, and teaching style to match the user's developmental stage. A user at an early stage of development receives simpler, more accessible guidance, while a more advanced user receives more subtle, nuanced, and direct teachings.

The chat interface supports both text and voice interaction. Users can type their questions and reflections or speak directly to the guide using voice input processed by Deepgram speech-to-text. The guide responds with both text and voice output generated by ElevenLabs text-to-speech. The voice is not a generic synthetic voice but a cloned or modeled voice that carries the characteristic qualities of the specific master being emulated. Voice responses are pre-warmed in a background cache system to minimize latency during conversations.

### Mirror Sessions

Mirror sessions are a distinctive feature of the application designed to help users see their own psychological patterns with greater clarity. During a mirror session, the system actively monitors the user's signals — including typing cadence, pause patterns, emotional language use, response timing, and when available, physiological signals from connected devices — to detect when a loop is activated or a pattern is playing out in real time.

The mirror does not simply observe silently. At strategic moments, it gently brings the user's attention to what it is noticing. For example, if the system detects the user's communication style shifting into a familiar people-pleasing pattern, the mirror might reflect this observation back with a question that invites the user to notice the pattern for themselves. This reflective function is designed to develop the user's capacity for self-observation, which is the foundational skill of the entire consciousness development path.

### Memory System

The application implements a four-layer memory architecture that allows the AI to remember what mattered across sessions. The first layer is session memory, which retains the key themes, discoveries, and emotional highlights from each individual session for the duration of a day. The second layer is short-term memory, spanning approximately seven days, which captures recent patterns, ongoing themes, and emotional trajectories. The third layer is long-term memory, which consolidates significant insights, breakthroughs, and developmental milestones over months and years. The fourth layer is pattern memory, which stores identified loops, their activation histories, intervention effectiveness, and developmental progress.

This memory system ensures that tomorrow's session is always informed by what happened today. If a user had a breakthrough in their morning session around a specific pattern of self-criticism, the evening session might offer a complementary practice that deepens that insight. If a user has been struggling with the same loop for three consecutive days, the adaptive engine may shift to a more direct intervention approach. The memory system makes the application genuinely responsive to the user's unique journey rather than delivering generic content.

### Consciousness Engine

The consciousness engine is the computational core that powers all personalization and adaptation in the application. It operates continuously, updating the user's dimensional profile, tracking V-Level progression signals, monitoring loop activation patterns, and computing readiness for different types of practices. The consciousness engine runs primarily on Supabase edge functions, which are serverless Deno functions deployed globally for low latency.

The engine receives user context snapshots that include identity information, current band classification, dimension scores across all tracked dimensions, active loop catalog entries, gate status (readiness thresholds for different practice types), current energy and mood indicators, theme preferences, signal data from integrations, foundation course completion status, current program enrollment, and the user's unique practice DNA. This comprehensive snapshot allows the engine to make nuanced decisions about session composition, guide selection, and content recommendations.

### Gamification and Progression

The application uses a sophisticated progression system that tracks development without gamifying it in a trivial sense. The V-Level system provides clear developmental milestones, while the H-Level capacities allow users to see their strengths and growth areas across different dimensions. The loop tracking system makes psychological patterns visible and measurable, turning invisible struggles into trackable, addressable phenomena.

Streak tracking encourages consistent practice with a grace period system that prevents guilt and shame when life circumstances prevent daily engagement. The application recognizes that forcing practice through guilt is counterproductive to genuine consciousness development, so the streak system includes built-in flexibility. Level progression unlocks new features, practices, and content areas, creating a sense of developmental narrative that motivates continued engagement.

The application also includes knowledge cards — concise wisdom teachings drawn from multiple traditions that users collect as they progress. These cards serve as portable reminders of insights and can be reviewed individually or in themed collections. The living glossary provides definitions of spiritual and psychological terminology used throughout the application, making the wisdom traditions accessible to users at all levels of familiarity.

### Community and Social Features

The application includes community features that allow users to connect with others on similar developmental paths. Peer circles provide small group spaces for shared practice and discussion. The enterprise version extends these community features into organizational settings with team challenges, group programs, and manager coaching dashboards. The constellation feature maps users' relational and developmental networks, visualizing connections between different aspects of their journey and progress.

### Enterprise Platform

Beyond individual users, SATYUG offers a comprehensive enterprise wellness platform designed for organizations that want to support the consciousness development of their employees. The enterprise platform includes employee profile management, team creation and administration, manager coaching dashboards that give leaders insights into their team's wellbeing and development patterns, an organizational mirror that provides a macro view of the company's collective consciousness landscape, peer circle facilitation tools, and a program designer that allows organizations to create custom wellness journeys tailored to their specific culture and needs.

Enterprise reporting includes people intelligence analytics, engagement tracking, wellbeing trend analysis, and ROI measurement. The enterprise platform is served through a separate administrative shell with dedicated routing, authentication, and feature sets distinct from the individual user experience.

## PART 4: TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

### Client-Side Technology Stack

The application frontend is built with React 18.3 and TypeScript 5.8, compiled and bundled with Vite 5 using the React SWC plugin for fast compilation. Routing is handled by React Router DOM version 6, providing a comprehensive nested route structure that supports both public-facing pages and authenticated application areas. The styling system combines Tailwind CSS 3.4 with the shadcn/ui component library, which provides over twenty Radix UI primitives for accessible, unstyled component foundations that are then styled with Tailwind utility classes. Animation and motion are handled by Framer Motion version 12, providing smooth transitions, page animations, and interactive feedback. Three.js version 0.170 together with the React Three Drei library powers the immersive 3D environments, including the cosmic backgrounds, the breathing orb visualization, temple environments, and particle effects.

State management on the client side is handled through two complementary systems. XState version 5.28 provides ten dedicated state machines that govern the major application flows. These state machines manage app entry and onboarding, the daily cycle of morning and evening practices, the home screen experience, practice session lifecycle, generic session orchestration, voice interactions, journaling flows, analytics and insights navigation, settings management, and notification handling. XState provides deterministic, debuggable state transitions that make complex multi-step flows predictable and testable.

The second state management layer is TanStack Query version 5.83, which handles all server state including user data, session history, practice catalogs, and dynamic content. TanStack Query is configured with IndexedDB persistence through custom persister adapters, enabling twenty-four-hour offline cache that allows the application to function without network connectivity. The persistence layer clears its cache automatically when a user switches accounts, preventing data leakage between users on shared devices.

### Internationalization

The application supports over sixty languages through a custom internationalization system. Locale files are organized in the src/locales directory with individual files for each supported language. The LanguageProvider context makes translations available throughout the component tree. The extensive language support reflects the application's intention to serve a global audience of spiritual seekers across diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds.

### State Machines

The ten XState state machines form the backbone of the application's interactive flows. Each machine is a self-contained statechart that defines all possible states, transitions, guards, and side effects for its domain. The app entry machine manages the complex onboarding flow, determining whether a user is new or returning, what authentication state they are in, and what initial experience they should receive. The daily cycle machine manages the transitions between morning practice, midday reflection, evening review, and night rest modes. The practice machine governs the lifecycle of an active practice session from selection through completion. The session machine provides a generic orchestration layer for all session types. The voice machine manages the states of voice interaction including listening, processing, and speaking. The journal machine governs the journaling flow from prompt selection through entry completion. Each machine is independently testable and can be visualized using XState's inspector tools during development.

The component library is organized into approximately ninety-seven directories, each representing a domain-specific component collection. The ui directory contains the shadcn/ui primitive components that provide the foundational design system. The cinematic directory contains the video player and intro sequence components. The session directory contains all components related to active practice experiences. The session shell directory contains the orchestration context and API layer that coordinates multi-component session experiences. The temple directory contains the 3D environment components including the temple model, cosmic orbs, and starfield backgrounds. The community directory contains social and group features. The enterprise directory contains B2B-specific layouts and components. The admin directory contains administrative interface components. The integrations directory contains clients for third-party services.

Custom hooks number approximately ninety-seven and provide domain-specific logic encapsulation. The useCompanionEngine hook orchestrates the AI companion behavior. The useConsciousnessEngine hook manages consciousness model interactions. The useLiveDetection hook handles real-time session signal processing. The useGuideChat hook manages the guide conversation interface. The useSessionFlow hook handles practice session lifecycle management. The usePracticeRun hook executes active practice logic. The useKarma hook tracks karmic patterns. The useNotifications hook manages push and local notifications. Each hook encapsulates its data fetching, state management, and side effects, providing a clean interface to the component layer.

The adaptiveJourneyEngine computes daily adaptations based on comprehensive user state analysis. The sessionEngine provides AI-directed session recommendations. The sessionOrchestrator coordinates multi-step session flows including transitions between different practice types within a single session. The sessionFlow manages the complete lifecycle of a session from initiation through completion and reflection.

The consciousness subsystem contains the implementations for computing V-Levels, updating dimension scores, detecting loop activations, and generating consciousness insights. The memory subsystem implements the four-layer memory architecture including extraction from sessions, embedding in vector stores, consolidation across time periods, and recall mechanisms. The mirrors subsystem implements the mirror session logic including signal detection, pattern recognition, and reflective intervention timing. The practice subsystem contains the engines for specific practice types including breathwork patterns, meditation timers, self-inquiry flows, and body awareness exercises.

The cinematic subsystem handles the rendering of immersive 3D environments, including environment selection, transition effects, lighting control, and audio-reactive visualizations. ### Supabase Backend

The backend infrastructure is built on Supabase, providing a managed PostgreSQL 15 database, authentication services, file storage, realtime subscriptions, and a global edge function network. The database schema includes over forty-two tables with comprehensive row-level security policies ensuring that users can only access their own data. The schema is managed through over one hundred thirty-four migration files that track all schema changes over time. TypeScript types are auto-generated from the database schema and committed to the repository, providing type safety across the full stack.

Supabase authentication handles user identity, session management, and access control. The application supports email and password authentication with plans for additional providers. Real-time subscriptions enable live updates to session data, notifications, and collaborative features without requiring manual refresh.

### Edge Functions

The application deploys one hundred one Supabase edge functions, which are serverless Deno functions executed at the edge for minimal latency. These functions implement the intelligence layer of the application, handling everything from session composition to memory processing to enterprise analytics. The edge functions are organized into several functional categories.

Session composition functions include the compose-session-v3 function which is the latest implementation of the session composer, the composer-engine which handles complex composition logic, and various generate-session functions for specialized session types. Intelligence functions include the consciousness-engine which computes V-Levels and dimension updates, the companion-engine which orchestrates the main AI companion behavior, the adaptive-journey and adapt-next-block functions which handle content adaptation, and various insight generation functions.

Memory functions include memory-extract which pulls key information from completed sessions, memory-consolidate which merges new memories with existing stored memories, recall-memory which retrieves relevant memories for current sessions, and embed-memory which creates vector embeddings for semantic search. Detection functions include live-detect which processes real-time signals during sessions, signal-fusion which combines multiple signal sources into unified state assessments, pattern-window which identifies recurring patterns across time, instant-pulse which provides immediate state detection, and local-loop-detector which runs client-side pattern detection.

Communication functions include guide-chat and temple-chat which handle conversational AI interactions, pre-journey-chat which manages initial user conversations, tts-speak which generates voice output through ElevenLabs, and stt-listen which processes voice input through Deepgram. Enterprise functions include enterprise-insights, enterprise-people-intelligence, org-program-designer, and manager-coaching which provide B2B-specific intelligence and tools. Admin functions include admin-ai-insights, admin-broadcast, and admin-copilot which provide administrative oversight and automation capabilities. Payment functions include razorpay-order and razorpay-verify which handle the payment processing flow.

Most edge functions are configured with JWT verification disabled, relying instead on the comprehensive row-level security policies in the database to enforce access control. This architectural decision simplifies the edge function code while maintaining security through the database layer.

### Composer Framework

The Composer framework is one of the most architecturally significant systems in the application. It implements a three-tier content assembly system that allows AI-generated session experiences. At the lowest level are atoms — indivisible units of session content such as a single inquiry question, a breathwork instruction, a moment of silence, or a brief teaching. Atoms are the fundamental building blocks and are not composed of smaller units.

At the middle level are ingredients, which are collections of atoms organized around a specific purpose or theme. For example, an ingredient might be a "body scan sequence" composed of ten to fifteen atoms that guide attention through different body regions. Another ingredient might be a "self-inquiry ladder" that walks the user through a series of progressively deeper questions. Ingredients are reusable across different sessions and archetypes.

At the highest level are archetypes, which are complete session templates that specify which ingredients should be used, in what sequence, with what timing, and under what conditions. An archetype might be a "Morning Awakening Session" that includes a specific opening sequence, a central inquiry ingredient, a breathwork ingredient, and a closing integration. Different archetypes serve different purposes — some are designed for deep inquiry, others for energetic activation, others for emotional processing, and others for rest and integration.

The Composer engine receives a user context snapshot and an archetype selection, then dynamically selects specific ingredients and atoms based on the user's current state, developmental level, and needs. This means that a "Morning Awakening Session" for a user in an early stage of development who is struggling with anxiety will be composed of completely different atoms than the same archetype for an advanced user who is ready for deep self-inquiry. The Composer framework is what makes every session unique and personalized rather than generic and pre-recorded.

### AI Gateway and Provider Abstraction

The application uses an AI Gateway abstraction that routes requests between different language model providers. The primary providers are Groq, which offers fast inference speeds suitable for real-time voice conversations, and Google Gemini, which provides high-quality reasoning for complex composition and analysis tasks. The gateway handles provider selection, request routing, error handling, fallback logic, and credit management.

The AI gateway is used for multiple purposes beyond chat completion. It generates embeddings for memory storage and retrieval, processes complex analysis tasks for the consciousness engine, generates session content and teaching material, powers the guide chat conversations, and drives the adaptive journey recommendations. The gateway includes credit management through the global credit guard system, which tracks API usage and prevents overrun of allocated budgets. When a provider quota is exhausted, the system gracefully degrades by switching to alternative providers or using cached responses.

### Signal Processing and Detection

The application incorporates a sophisticated signal processing system that monitors multiple input channels to infer the user's current psychological and physiological state. During practice sessions, the system monitors typing cadence and rhythm when the user is interacting with text inputs, pause patterns that may indicate emotional processing or resistance, the emotional language used in responses, response timing relative to prompts, and when available, physiological signals from connected wearable devices.

External integrations extend the signal collection beyond the application itself. When users grant consent, the system can access calendar data to understand upcoming stressors or commitments, communication patterns in email and messaging to detect relationship dynamics, meeting tone analysis to understand workplace stress levels, and wearable data including heart rate variability for physiological state assessment. The signal fusion engine combines all available signals into a unified state assessment that feeds into the consciousness engine and adaptive journey system.

The live detection system processes these signals in real time during sessions, enabling the mirror function to identify loop activations as they happen. The pattern window system looks across longer time horizons to identify patterns that may not be visible in real-time signal analysis. The local loop detector runs on the client device for immediate pattern recognition without requiring network round trips.

## PART 6: DATA FLOW AND SYSTEM INTERACTIONS

### User Context Snapshot

The central data structure that drives personalization throughout the application is the user context snapshot. This snapshot is a comprehensive aggregation of everything the system knows about a user at a given moment. It includes identity information such as user ID, name, and account details. It includes the user's current band or V-Level classification. It includes dimension scores across all tracked dimensions of experience. It includes the user's active loop catalog entries with activation histories. It includes gate status information that determines what types of practices the user is ready for. It includes current energy and mood indicators derived from recent signals. It includes theme preferences and aesthetic choices. It includes signal data from all connected integrations. It includes foundation course completion status. It includes current program enrollment and progress. It includes the user's practice DNA, which is a unique profile of how they respond to different practice types.

This snapshot is gathered at the beginning of every session and sent to the Composer engine and consciousness engine via edge functions. The snapshot is also used by the adaptive journey engine to determine daily content, by the guide selection algorithm to match users with appropriate guides, and by the memory system to contextualize new experiences within the user's developmental history.

### Session Composition Flow

When a user initiates a practice session, a complex multi-step flow begins. First, the session shell component gathers the user context snapshot from various sources including the Supabase database, local storage, TanStack Query cache, and real-time subscriptions. This snapshot is then sent to the compose-session-v3 edge function along with the selected session archetype and any specific user intentions or requests.

The edge function runs the Composer engine, which selects appropriate ingredients based on the user's V-Level, active loops, energy state, and time of day. The Composer then sequences the selected ingredients into a coherent session flow, determining timing for each section, transitions between practices, and integration moments. The composed session is returned to the client along with a unique session ID and metadata.

The session shell then orchestrates the playback of the composed session, coordinating the 3D environment rendering, audio playback, guide voice generation, real-time signal monitoring, and user interaction handling. During the session, the live detection system monitors user signals and can trigger mid-session adaptations if significant patterns are detected. At the end of the session, the session data is sent to the memory extraction function to capture key moments and insights for future sessions.

### Memory Pipeline

The memory pipeline processes completed sessions through multiple stages. First, the memory-extract function analyzes the session data to identify key themes, insights, emotional moments, and pattern activations. This extracted information is formatted into a structured memory object. The embed-memory function then creates a vector embedding of the memory, allowing it to be semantically searched and related to other memories.

The memory-consolidate function periodically reviews recent memories and integrates them with existing long-term memories, identifying cross-session patterns and developmental themes. This consolidation process updates the user's loop activation histories, dimension scores, and developmental insights. The recall-memory function is called at the beginning of each new session to retrieve relevant memories that should inform the upcoming session's composition.

### Adaptive Journey Engine

The adaptive journey engine operates at a higher level than the session composer, managing the user's multi-day and multi-week developmental trajectory. Rather than composing individual sessions, the adaptive engine determines what types of sessions the user needs over time, what programs would be most beneficial, when to introduce new practices, and when to deepen existing practices.

The engine considers the user's progression through V-Levels, the status of their active loops, their engagement patterns, feedback from recent sessions, and their stated intentions. It operates on a block-based system where the journey is divided into blocks of days or weeks, and each block has a specific developmental focus. The adapt-next-block function determines what the next block should focus on based on the outcomes of the current block.

### Onboarding Flow

New users enter the application through a carefully designed onboarding flow managed by the app entry state machine. The flow begins with a cinematic introduction that establishes the sacred tone of the application. Users are then guided through an initial assessment that gathers information about their background, experience with contemplative practices, current challenges, and developmental intentions.

The onboarding includes an explanation of the consciousness model, introduction to the three pillars of practice, and a first practice session that is carefully calibrated to the user's starting point. The onboarding flow adapts based on user responses, offering different paths for complete beginners versus experienced practitioners, different approaches for those seeking emotional healing versus those seeking spiritual development, and different pacing for those with limited time versus those able to commit to intensive practice.

## PART 10: DEVELOPMENT AND DEPLOYMENT

### Build System

The application uses Vite 5 as its build tool, configured with the React SWC plugin for fast compilation. The Vite configuration includes path aliasing with the @ symbol pointing to the src directory, simplifying imports throughout the codebase. Heavy dependencies such as Radix UI primitives are pre-bundled to prevent cold-load HMR race conditions during development. The build process emits a version.json file containing a timestamp-based build ID that enables mobile cache busting when new versions are deployed. Chunk size warnings are configured at twelve hundred kilobytes to alert developers when bundles grow too large.

The development server hosts on all network interfaces at port eight thousand eighty, with the HMR overlay disabled to provide a cleaner development experience. The production build optimizes for minimal initial load through code splitting, with all non-critical routes lazy-loaded and prefetched in the background during idle time.

### Code Organization

The codebase follows a feature-based organization pattern where code is grouped by domain rather than by type. The hooks directory contains all custom React hooks organized by domain. The lib directory contains all business logic organized by functional area. The components directory contains all UI components organized by feature area. The data directory contains all static content organized by content type. This organization makes it easy to locate code related to a specific feature and understand the full implementation of that feature in one place.

### Testing

The application uses Vitest 3.2 as its testing framework, configured in vitest.config.ts. The test directory contains unit tests for key logic including AI context handling, graph algorithms, and mirror adaptive behavior. The testing library includes React Testing Library and Jest DOM matchers for component testing, with JSDOM providing a browser-like environment for tests. Tests can be run in single-run mode through npm test or in watch mode through npm run test:watch for active development.

### Linting and Code Quality

ESLint is configured with a flat config format in eslint.config.js, providing code quality enforcement across the codebase. The linting rules enforce TypeScript best practices, React patterns, accessibility standards, and consistent code style. The npm run lint command runs the full linting suite, and linting is typically integrated into the development workflow and CI/CD pipeline.

### Deployment Pipeline

The application is deployed to Vercel, configured through the vercel.json file. The Vercel configuration specifies the Vite framework preset, the dist output directory, and SPA fallback rewrites that route all non-file requests to index.html for client-side routing. Strict content security policy headers are configured to allow connections to required external services including Supabase, Groq, Gemini, ElevenLabs, and Deepgram while blocking all other external resource loads.

Additional security headers include X-Frame-Options set to deny to prevent clickjacking, X-Content-Type-Options set to nosniff to prevent MIME type confusion attacks, HSTS headers to enforce HTTPS connections, and a strict referrer policy to prevent information leakage through referrer headers.

### Environment Configuration

The application uses environment variables for all configuration that varies between deployment environments. Client-side environment variables are prefixed with VITE_ and are exposed to the browser through Vite's build-time substitution. These include the Supabase URL, publishable key, project ID, and Razorpay key ID. Server-side environment variables are managed as Supabase secrets and are only accessible within edge functions. These include API keys for Groq, Gemini, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Razorpay, and various integration services.

A .env file stores local development configuration, while .env.secrets contains sensitive values that are git-ignored. The .env.example file provides a template for required environment variables, making it easy for new developers to set up the project.

### Mobile Deployment

Mobile deployment is handled through Capacitor 8.3, which wraps the web application in native iOS and Android containers. The Capacitor configuration specifies the app bundle identifier, the web directory pointing to the production dist output, and plugin configurations including the health plugin for accessing device health data. The iOS and Android platforms can be built and deployed through standard Capacitor commands, with the application distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store respectively.

### PWA Configuration

The application is configured as a Progressive Web App through the vite-plugin-pwa package. PWA configuration includes service worker caching strategies, install prompts, and web app manifest metadata. The manifest defines the application name, icons, theme colors, display mode, and start URL. PWA capabilities allow users to install the application on their home screens on both mobile and desktop platforms, providing an app-like experience without requiring native app store distribution.

### The IP Moat

The project's architecture documentation explicitly identifies the consciousness model as the core intellectual property and competitive moat of the business. The consciousness model identifies twenty-two vertical bands of development that provide finer granularity than the seven V-Levels alone. These bands represent developmental nuances within each V-Level, allowing for more precise personalization and progression tracking. The band system ensures that users at the same general stage but with different specific developmental characteristics receive appropriately differentiated content and guidance.

The four hundred loops in the catalog are not merely categorized but are individually defined with specific trigger conditions, emotional signatures, behavioral expressions, root cause analyses, and mapped intervention strategies. ### Designed for Obsolescence

Perhaps the most unusual aspect of the business model is the explicit design goal of becoming unnecessary to the user over time. The application measures success not by retention metrics but by the user's increasing independence from the application. As users develop the capacities of observation, self-inquiry, and self-release, they need less external guidance and can maintain their practice and awareness independently. This "anti-engagement" model inverts the typical SaaS retention imperative, aligning the application's incentives with the user's genuine development rather than artificial engagement metrics.
