# 6. User Persona Journey — A Day in the Life

**Meet Aria.** She found the app through a `/blog` post about "spiritual bypassing" (SEO funnel — `Blog.tsx`/`BlogPost.tsx`), then clicked through to the public **Loop Library** at `/loops` (`Loops.tsx`) out of curiosity about a "people-pleasing" pattern. From there she tried the no-login **Showroom** at `/experience` — a guided "self-inquiry" mini-session (`ExperienceSelfInquiry.tsx` → `SelfInquiryLiveSession.tsx`) that let her feel the product's voice before committing to an account. Convinced, she hits **`/auth`** and signs up.

**First minutes — AppEntry & onboarding.** `AppEntry.tsx` boots the `appEntryMachine`: her session resolves, `profiles.onboarded` is `false`, so she's routed into `QuickOnboarding` — four fast questions rather than the deep 80-session path. On completion, `ONBOARDING_COMPLETE` flips the machine to `ready` and `AppLayout` mounts for the first time. Behind the scenes, an `app_open` karma event is throttled-awarded (`useAwardKarmaEvent`), and a `user_consciousness_profile` row now exists with an `assigned_guru_id` computed from her answers (e.g. "Aarav") and an `overall_band` seeding her starting practice level.

**Morning — Home & the Orb.** She lands on `/app` → `Home.tsx`. `useThemeOfDay()` fetches today's `daily_engine_plan`-derived theme; `useConsciousnessEngine('morning')` picks today's guru voice. The screen shows: a streak flame, a karma chip, a theme card, and — the centerpiece — the breathing `DailyOrb`. She presses and holds for 1.5 seconds (a small ritual of intention baked into the interaction itself) and is dropped into a full-screen **Guide session** (`Guide.tsx`), whose `sessionMachine` moves `idle → starting` (a `sessions` row is created, `SESSION_CREATED` fires) `→ active`. The guide's opening line streams in via the typewriter-smoothed `STREAM_DELTA` pipeline — she can *feel* it thinking as it types, at a natural ~110 chars/sec. Partway through, the guide directs her into a breathing practice: `useCinematicSession().play({practice:'pranayama', userId})` fires, `pickScript()` resolves the `pranayama` script and looks up her `user_practice_constellation.pranayama_level` (still 0, brand new) to pick **Layer 0** — maximum hand-holding, slow timed beats, one gentle Yes/Not-yet checkpoint. A full-screen `CinematicSession` overlay takes over (outside the normal route tree, mounted by the `CinematicSessionProvider` in `AppLayout`), guiding her breath with silent timed pauses between lines. When it completes, `onComplete()` resumes the Guide session, which wraps up and `END_SESSION → SESSION_ENDED → completed`. A `session_messages` transcript now exists; `extract-sacred-moments` will later mine it for anything worth remembering.

**Midday — a nudge, not a session.** She's at work. A calendar-watcher-style ambient signal (if she'd opted into workplace integrations — she hasn't yet, so `signal_consents` stays empty) would otherwise feed the engine; for Aria today it's simply the `useIdleNudge`/`useJourneyNudge` background hooks in `AppLayout` that, hours after her morning session, trigger a gentle push notification via `usePushNotifications` — a `notifications` row is inserted server-side, her `NotificationBell` realtime channel fires `NEW_NOTIFICATION`, and the bell badge appears live without her refreshing anything.

**Evening — Journal.** She opens the **Journal** tab (`Journal.tsx`). The `journalMachine` starts in `viewing`; tapping "new entry" sends `OPEN_FORM → composing`. She writes about noticing herself people-pleasing at a meeting today — a direct echo of the loop she read about before ever signing up. `SET_CONTENT` updates context live; because `hasContent` guard passes, `SUBMIT → saving` fires her `useMutation` write to `journal_entries` (`user_id`, `content`, optional `mood` tag toggled via `TOGGLE_MOOD`), and on success `ENTRY_SAVED` returns her to `viewing` with the form cleared. Server-side, `journal-reflect` may later analyze this entry, potentially feeding `memory_patterns` or nudging `active_loops` if the "people-pleasing" pattern is now considered "live" again — and if it's emotionally charged enough, `extract-sacred-moments` could tag it as a `realization` moment surfaced later in **Memory Vault** (`MemoryVault.tsx`) alongside her `breakthrough`/`gratitude`/`vow` moments, filterable by type and searchable.

**Night — closing the day.** Before bed she opens **Guide** once more for an evening reflection flow — the `evening_reflections` table captures mood/energy/gratitudes/highlights/challenges/lessons for the day, one row per `(user_id, reflection_date)`. `dailyCycleMachine`'s `EVENING_REFLECTED` event fires, `eveningReflected` flips true, and her streak/gate UI updates accordingly. The app's ambient background — driven by the `--slot-night-sky` CSS variables (deep indigo, low-saturation radial gradient) — has already shifted the whole app's mood without her doing anything, matching the literal time of day.

**Over the following weeks — Loops, Constellation, and the Foundation.** Aria never explicitly "enrolled" in the Foundation Journey — `usePreJourney()`'s auto-enroll effect quietly created her enrollment on `'balanced'` pace the first time she visited `PreJourney.tsx` (which she reaches by tapping a `QuickPathMirrorButton` surfaced on Home, since she's on the quick-onboarding path and the deep Foundation banner itself is hidden per current product direction). Every so often the floating Mirror button invites her to a **Mirror checkpoint** — a reflective self-assessment logged to `pre_journey_mirrors`/`mirror_answers`, contributing toward the eventual 32-mirror, 60-session **Cinematic Reveal** she'll experience months from now. Meanwhile, each pranayama/witness/self-inquiry session she completes nudges her `user_practice_constellation` levels upward; `compute-constellation`/`recalibrate-constellation` occasionally recompute her standing, and when her `pranayama_level` crosses the threshold from 0/1 to 2/3, the *very next* time she triggers that cinematic script, `resolveLayer()` silently promotes her to **Layer 1** — less hand-holding, more space between beats — without her ever visiting a "settings" screen to unlock it. She visits `/app/constellation` (`Constellation.tsx`) periodically to see this visualized, and `/app/loops` (inside the app, distinct from the public marketing `/loops`) to check whether her people-pleasing loop has moved from "active" to "regressed" to eventually "dissolved," per `regression_events`/`active_loops` bookkeeping.

**If Aria were an enterprise user instead:** her employer would have provisioned an `organizations` row and invited her via `org_invites`; she'd land on `/enterprise/auth` instead of `/auth`, her manager would see aggregated (never individually-identifying, per `signal_consents`) `mood_pulses` and `org_consciousness_snapshots` in `EnterpriseDashboard.tsx`, and she'd optionally connect Slack/Gmail/Calendar through the `SignalConsentCenter` (`/app/integrations`) to let ambient workplace-signal watchers (`gmail-tone-watcher`, `calendar-meeting-watcher`, etc.) feed her personal engine — the same `daily_engine_plans`/`consciousness_signals` tables Aria's morning theme was already built from, just with richer upstream signal.

**Full-circle**: everything Aria did — the loop she almost forgot from a blog post, the pranayama layer she unknowingly leveled up in, the journal entry that became a Memory Vault "realization," the mirror answer from three weeks ago — is stitched together by the consciousness engine into tomorrow morning's theme, chosen guru voice, and orb-tap session, closing the loop (pun intended) between the database schema (§1), the state machines that model each screen's micro-interactions (§2), the routes that string those screens together (§3), the sacred-obsidian-and-gold visual language that makes it feel like a temple rather than a to-do app (§4), and the streaming/credit/realtime plumbing that makes it all feel alive and responsive (§5).
