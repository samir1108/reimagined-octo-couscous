## 3. VOICE LAWS (non-negotiable)

### 3.1 Person & tone
- **70% second-person.** ("You open the app at 11:47 PM…")
- **30% third-person stories.** ("M, day 41…") — used for pain, failure, regression, dark nights.
- **0% first-person plural.** No "we believe." That signals committee.
- **Signed on LinkedIn:** `— Samir` (always). **Instagram:** brand-anonymous, founder face 1 in 10.

### 3.2 The pulse
- Honest > clever.
- Specific > universal. Name the hour. Name the app. Name the feeling.
- Quiet > loud. We are the antidote to noise. We don't add to it.
- One sharp truth > five soft platitudes.
- Brand name max **1× per post.** Lead with the human. End with the brand.

### 3.3 The Forbidden List (never appears, ever)
- "In today's fast-paced world…"
- "Many of us struggle with…"
- "Studies show…" (unless cited inline)
- "It's important to remember…"
- "Take a moment to…"
- "Trust the process" / "Let go" / "You got this"
- "Manifest" / "vibrate higher" / "raise your frequency"
- Any sentence that could appear on a beige Instagram quote tile.
- Any caption that doesn't name a specific moment, hour, or feeling.
- Emojis as decoration. (Functional only. Max 1 per post.)
- Hashtag walls. Max 5 hashtags. All lowercase.

### 3.4 The North Star Test (run before publishing)
1. Would Shiva on Kailash approve this?
2. Would the user on her 13th day not yelling at her son feel served by this?
3. Is this true at 11:47 PM on someone's worst night?
4. Did I name the moment, the hour, the feeling?

If any answer is no — rewrite.

## 6. POST ARCHITECTURE (every post follows this skeleton)

### 6.1 LinkedIn post (5 parts)
1. **The Hook** — one specific moment, hour, or number. (e.g. "Day 87. No revenue.")
2. **The Scene** — 2-4 sentences. Sensory. Concrete.
3. **The Turn** — the unexpected truth or decision.
4. **The Lesson** — one sentence. Not preachy. A founder's note to himself.
5. **Signature** — `— Samir` + (rare) one line: "Building ConsciousOS — [link in bio]"

### 6.2 Instagram carousel (5-7 slides)
- **Slide 1:** Hook — one line, max 8 words, white text on obsidian-black.
- **Slide 2-3:** The scene / the loop / the moment.
- **Slide 4-5:** The diagnosis (name the loop, the dimension, the hour).
- **Slide 6:** The shift — what changes when you see it.
- **Slide 7:** "Follow for the rest of the 300-day journey." + day counter.

### 6.3 Instagram reel (15-30s)
- 0-3s: pattern interrupt + named moment ("2:14 AM. Refreshing email #7.")
- 3-15s: scene + name the loop
- 15-25s: the turn (the truth)
- 25-30s: the brand exit ("The Temple is open. [day X / 300]")
