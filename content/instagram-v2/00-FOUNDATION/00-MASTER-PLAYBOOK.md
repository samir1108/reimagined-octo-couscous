## 1. THE FIVE LAWS (no exceptions, ever)

### Law 1 — One human, one moment, one hour
Every piece is aimed at **one** of the four protagonists (Samir / Arjun / Priya / Diya), at **one** specific moment, at **one** specific hour. Never "people." Never "viewers." Never "we." One person. One night. One screen.

### Law 2 — The hook is the screenshot
The first frame must be screenshotable as a still and still hit. If the still doesn't earn a save on its own — the hook is dead.

### Law 3 — Specificity is the moat
Every piece must contain at least **three** of: a real time (2:14 AM), a real number (₹42L, 9h 47m screen time), a real object (cold dal-chawal, charcoal hoodie), a real place (Bangalore 1BHK, hostel mess, Marine Drive), a real loop ID (AC003, CAR012). Vague = dead.

### Law 4 — Silence is louder than sound
Every reel must contain at least one deliberate silence (≥1.5s, ideally a beat of pure ambient/breath). The silence is the moment the viewer feels seen. Without it, you have a TikTok, not a sutra.

### Law 5 — Name the loop in HUMAN language (codes stay backstage)
Every piece is anchored to a real loop from the library — but the **loop ID never appears on screen and is never spoken**. IDs (VAL004, CMP008, AVD133, etc.) live ONLY in: the brief, the filename, the production receipt, and our analytics. On-screen and in-voice we name the loop the way the protagonist would feel it: *"the 2 AM refresh"*, *"the empty-room mother"*, *"the four-productivity-apps trap"*. The ID is the spine. The human name is the skin.

## 2. THE 3-SECOND TEST (run this on every piece)

Watch the first 3 seconds with sound OFF. The viewer must think one of:

1. *"Wait — how did they know?"*
2. *"This is literally me at 2 AM yesterday."*
3. *"I've never heard anyone say this out loud."*
4. *"Oh god, I thought I was the only one."*
5. *"Send this to [specific friend] right now."*

If none fire → **kill it.**

## 3. CRAFT GATES — what world-class actually means

Every piece is graded across these 7 axes. **Average must be ≥9/10. No single axis below 8.** Otherwise — back to the bench.

| # | Axis | What 10/10 looks like |
|---|------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Hook** | Screenshotable. No logo, no title card, no warmup. The moment IS the opener. |
| 2 | **Specificity** | At least 3 concrete anchors (time/number/object/place/loop). Zero generics. |
| 3 | **Voice** | Sounds like a human texting one person at 11:47 PM. Not a brand. Not a coach. |
| 4 | **Visual** | Pixar/Soul-grade keyframes. Cinematic LUT. No stock-photo energy. |
| 5 | **Captions** | Anton 140pt, all-caps, gold (#FFD700) word-by-word kinetic. Positioned at **52% from top** (above thumb-zone, never bottom-ugly). Max 3 words per beat. Black 6px shadow. **Never** auto-generated default style. |
| 6 | **Audio** | VO ducked clean against music bed (-22dB). One deliberate silence ≥1.5s. ElevenLabs voice matched to protagonist (not narrator-generic). |
| 7 | **Closing** | Names the loop. Names the next move. Ends with a specific human action ("DM me." / "Tag a mother." / "Screenshot this. Send it to one person."). Never "follow for more." |

## 4. CAPTION SPEC (locked — anyone breaking this is rebuilding it)

This is where most "AI content" dies. Ours doesn't.

```
font:        Anton (Bold), 140pt
case:        ALL CAPS
fill:        #FFFFFF
highlight:   #FFD700 (gold) — current spoken word ONLY
shadow:      6px, #000000, opacity 95%
stroke:      4px black outline (so it survives any background)
position:    Y = 82% of frame height (BOTTOM-ANCHORED, above IG's UI bar)
             — sits just below the protagonist's face, above the like/share row
alignment:   center
max words:   3 per beat (one "card" per ~0.6–1.0s)
animation:   word-by-word reveal, 80ms ease-in scale 0.92→1.0
margin-x:    96px (frame edges sacred — never touch)
safe-zone:   nothing in top 12% (IG username) or bottom 12% (caption/CTA UI)
             captions live in the bottom-third band: Y=78%–86%
forbidden:   loop IDs in caption text (no "VAL004", no "CMP008") — human names only
```

If you see captions auto-burned by CapCut defaults, mid-frame, or running >3 words per line — **the piece is not done.**

## 7. THE PRODUCTION PIPELINE (locked, repeatable)

Every reel = the same 7-step pipeline. No improvisation.

```
1. BRIEF       Markdown brief: protagonist, loop, 6-shot storyboard,
                VO script with pause map, music mood, hook frame.
2. KEYFRAMES   imagegen × 5–6 (Pixar/Soul aesthetic, character sheet locked)
3. CLIPS       videogen × 5–6 (10s each, starting_frame = keyframe)
4. VOICE       ElevenLabs, voice matched to protagonist code,
                stability/style per voice spec
5. MUSIC       ElevenLabs Sound-Gen — lo-fi/ambient/Indo-jazz, 60s, -22dB
6. CAPTIONS    Groq Whisper transcribe → ASS subtitle generator
                (Anton 140pt / gold word highlight / Y=52%)
7. COMPOSE     ffmpeg: clips concat → music duck → VO mix → caption burn
                → 1080×1920 H.264 high-bitrate MP4
```

Every piece carries a `production-receipt.json` listing: keyframes used, voice ID, music seed, caption template version, ffmpeg command. Reproducibility is non-negotiable.

## 8. THE QA RITUAL (before any post leaves the build)

Three watches. Different conditions. All must pass.

1. **Sound OFF, phone screen** — captions readable, hook visible, story still legible.
2. **Sound ON, headphones** — silence lands, voice cuts through, music doesn't compete.
3. **Sound ON, phone speaker** — voice intelligible at 60% volume in a noisy room.

Then the **send-to-one-friend test**: Would Samir send this to his ex-CTO without flinching? If no → it's not done.

## 9. THE 250-PIECE STRATEGY (why we win)

We don't post 250 pieces. We post 250 **diagnoses**.

Each piece names a loop someone is *currently inside* and lets them feel caught — gently, accurately, without shame. The compounding effect:

- Piece 1: *"How did they know?"* → save
- Piece 7: *"They keep knowing."* → follow
- Piece 22: *"They named the thing my therapist couldn't."* → DM
- Piece 50: *"This brand sees me."* → install + tell three friends
- Piece 100: *"I quote them."* → 10M trajectory

**We are not building reach. We are building recognition.** Reach is the side effect.
