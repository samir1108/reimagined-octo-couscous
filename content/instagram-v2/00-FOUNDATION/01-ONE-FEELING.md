# THE ONE FEELING

> **"This is my story. This is for me. This is what I was waiting for."**

This sentence is written on top of every single one of the 415 files in this library.

Every voice post, every reel, every carousel must pass ONE test before it ships:

**Does the viewer feel seen?**

Not entertained. Not informed. Not impressed.

**Seen.**

---

## The 3-Second Test

Within 3 seconds of any piece, the right viewer must think one of these thoughts:

1. *"Wait — how did they know?"*
2. *"This is literally me at 2am yesterday."*
3. *"I've never heard anyone say this out loud."*
4. *"Oh god, I thought I was the only one."*
5. *"Send this to [specific friend] right now."*

If none of those fire — the piece is a draft, not a post.

## The 4 People We Are Writing For

Every single piece must be aimed at ONE of these four humans like a laser. Not "everyone." Not "people who like meditation." **One specific person at one specific moment.**

### 1. **The Burnt-Out Engineer** (Arjun, 28, Bangalore/Bengaluru/Pune)
- Senior at a series-B startup. Last promotion: 14 months ago.
- Opens Slack at 11:47 PM "just to check one thing."
- Hasn't called his mother in 3 weeks.
- Tried Headspace. Tried Calm. Both feel like flossing — knows he should, doesn't.
- Real wound: *"I'm building something that doesn't matter, and I can't stop."*

### 2. **The Invisible Mother** (Priya, 34, Mumbai/Delhi/Hyderabad)

- The last time someone asked her how *she* was doing, she cried in the car.

- Real wound: *"I am disappearing inside my own life and nobody noticed."*

### 3. **The Lost Student** (Diya, 21, Pune/Bangalore/anywhere)

- Every Instagram post makes her feel further behind.

### 4. **The Founder Who Can't Sleep** (Samir, 26, Bangalore — this is us)

## The Voice Rule

Write every piece as if you are texting **one of those four people at 11:47 PM on their worst night.**

Not "users." Not "followers." Not "the audience."

**That one person. That one night. That one screen.**

If you find yourself writing in the third person ("People often feel..."), delete and restart.

---

## The Forbidden List

Things that will NEVER appear in this library:

- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Many of us struggle with..."
- "Studies show..."  *(unless cited inline with a real study)*
- "It's important to remember..."
- "Take a moment to..."
- "Trust the process"
- "Let go"
- "You got this"
- "Manifest" / "vibrate higher" / "raise your frequency"
- Any sentence that could appear on a beige Instagram quote tile
- Any caption that doesn't name a specific moment, hour, or feeling

---

## The North Star Test (Run This Before Posting Anything)

Read the piece. Then ask yourself:

1. Could this have been written by ChatGPT for any wellness brand? → **Kill it.**
2. Could a 28-year-old engineer screenshot this and send it to his best friend? → **Ship it.**
3. Would Samir (the founder) be embarrassed if his ex-CTO saw it because it's too generic? → **Kill it.**
4. Would Samir send this to his own mother because it finally explains him? → **Ship it.**
