## Recording Protocol For Samir's Real Voice (V1)

**Where:** Your bedroom at 11:47 PM. Lights off. Phone face down.
**Mic:** iPhone Voice Memos. Hold it 6 inches from your mouth.
**How:** Read each script ONCE without rehearsing. Then read it again. Use the second take 80% of the time. The first take is too "trying."
**What to keep:** Breaths. Pauses. The catch in your voice on the line that's actually true. The slight hoarseness. Don't edit it out. **Those are the brand.**
**What to cut:** Coughs, phone notifications, only. Nothing else.

## The Pause Map (every voice post specifies pauses)

Marked in scripts as:
- `(.)` — half-beat pause (~0.4s)
- `(..)` — one beat (~0.8s)
- `(...)` — two beats (~1.5s) — for emphasis
- `(....)` — three beats (~2.5s) — for the gut-punch line
- `(silence — 5s)` — full silence with just breath/music — for the "this is me" moment

**Every voice post must have at least one `(....)` pause and one `(silence — 5s)`. The silences are where people feel seen.**
