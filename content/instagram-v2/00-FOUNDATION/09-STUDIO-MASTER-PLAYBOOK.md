## 5. THE 5 LAWS (every piece, no exceptions — from Master Playbook §1)

1. **One human, one moment, one hour.** Never "people," never "we."
2. **The hook is the screenshot.** Frame 1 must save on its own.
3. **Specificity is the moat.** ≥3 anchors per piece (real time / real number / real object / real place / real loop).
4. **Silence is louder than sound.** Every reel ≥1.5s of pure silence/breath.
5. **Name the loop in HUMAN language.** Loop IDs (VAL004, EGO001) live in filenames + receipts ONLY. On screen: "the 2 AM refresh."

If a piece breaks any law → **kill it.** Not edit. Kill and start again.

## 7. THE STUDIO PIPELINE (one factory, repeatable for all 415)

```
1. BRIEF (markdown)            — protagonist, loop, 6-shot board, VO+pause map, music mood
2. CHARACTER LOCK              — pull lock sheet for protagonist (one-time per character)
3. KEYFRAMES × 6               — imagegen, "off lock sheet, same face, same wardrobe"
4. CLIPS × 5                   — videogen, starting_frame = matching keyframe
5. VOICEOVER                   — ElevenLabs, voice ID per protagonist, pause map honored
6. MUSIC BED                   — ElevenLabs sound-gen, 22s, looped to 60s, ducked −18 dB
7. CAPTIONS (.ass)             — Anton 140pt gold word highlight @ Y=82%, NEVER autoburn
8. FFMPEG COMPOSE              — concat → duck → mix → burn → 1080×1920 H.264 MP4
9. 11-GATE QA                  — sound off, sound on headphones, sound on phone speaker
10. PRODUCTION RECEIPT (.json) — every input, voice ID, music seed, ffmpeg cmd
```

Same 10 steps. Every piece. **Reproducibility is law.**

## 11. CREDIT-EFFICIENCY RULES (how we save without losing quality)

- **Reuse keyframes** — opening + closing shot = same frame, different beat. Saves 3 cr/reel.
- **5 clips not 6** — the 6th frame holds as a still on the closing line. Saves 5 cr/reel.
- **Music = 22s gen, looped via ffmpeg** to 60s. Saves repeat sound-gen calls.
- **Lock sheets reused 20+ times** — one 3 cr generation amortizes across an entire wave.
- **Voice posts share images** — one image used across 3–4 voice posts with different scripts. Saves 8 cr/piece.

Result: planned ~45 cr/reel ships at ~38 cr actual after the lock sheets are built.
