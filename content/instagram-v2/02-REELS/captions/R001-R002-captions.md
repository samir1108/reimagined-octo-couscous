## R002 — "The 2 AM Message"

**File:** `R002-the-2am-message.mp4` (45s, 9:16, 1080×1920)
**Protagonist:** Arjun (engineer, 28, Bangalore)
**Voice:** V2 (Aarav, ElevenLabs Charlie)
**Loop named:** AC003 — Achievement Loop

### Hook
> It's 2:14 AM.
> You're refreshing the same Slack message for the 7th time.
> Nobody is going to reply.
> You know.

### Body
There's a name for it.

**AC003 — the Achievement Loop.**

Your brain mistakes anxiety for productivity. You feel like you're working, but you're just feeding the loop.

Willpower won't break it.
Sleep hygiene won't break it.
Another productivity app won't break it.

What breaks it is the thing nobody teaches you to want:

**One person. Three words. "You up?"**

That's it. That's the whole jailbreak.

You don't break loops alone. You break them in conversation.
Even at 2 AM.
Especially at 2 AM.

— If this is you tonight, screenshot it. Send it to one person. That's the practice.
