# R001 — "The Rent Receipt"
**Samir. 2:47 AM. ₹52,000.**

A 26-year-old founder, alone in a Bangalore 1BHK, opens his banking app at 2:47 AM. Rent auto-debited. ₹52,000 gone. Account: ₹8,400 left. He doesn't cry. He just sits. And then he builds the thing he has been afraid to build for fourteen months.

This is not a pitch. This is the minute Conscious OS started.
