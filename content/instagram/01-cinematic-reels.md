# Instagram — Cinematic Reels (30 scripts × 30s)

Format per reel: **Hook (0–3s) → Build (3–22s) → Land (22–28s) → Caption tag (28–30s)**
Visual universe: black, gold, single human silhouette, mountain, flame, lotus. No stock footage.
Voiceover: cloned guru voice (Krishna, Buddha, Ramana, Modern Guru) via ElevenLabs.
On-screen text: max 6 words per cut. Cormorant Garamond italic for emotion. Cinzel for declarations.

---

## R1 — "The first technology was not the wheel"
- **0–3s** Black. A single ember ignites center-frame.
- **3–10s** VO (Modern Guru, low): *"Before fire was named, before language hardened, a being sat on a mountain — and stopped moving."*
- **10–18s** Camera pulls back. Silhouette on Kailash, dawn breaking. Dust motes in gold.
- **18–25s** Text fades up: *"Not stopped working. Stopped moving."*
- **25–30s** Cut to black. One line: *"The original technology. Returned."* Logo flame.
- **Caption:** Yoga is not stretching. It is the oldest technology we ever invented. We forgot. The fire is being relit.
