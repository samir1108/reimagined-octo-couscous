# Instagram — Voice-Only Reels (20 × 15–45s)

Format: black screen + slow-drifting gold particles. Voiceover (cloned guru voice via ElevenLabs). One line of text appears at the very end. Production cost: zero. Emotional cost: maximum.

---

## V1 — Krishna voice, 20s
*"What if you have been running so fast toward what you want — that you have been running away from the only person who can actually have it?"*
End text: **stop. for one breath.**

---

## V2 — Buddha voice, 25s
*"You think you have anxiety. You have a loop. You think you have depression. You have a loop. You think you are lazy. You have a loop. The you that is none of these things is what is reading this sentence."*
End text: **the loop is not you.**

---

## V3 — Ramana voice, 18s
*"You came here to ask a question. I will not answer it. I will only ask: who is the one asking? Sit with that. Everything else is noise."*
End text: **who is asking?**

---

## V4 — Modern Guru, 30s
*"Your phone has trained you to expect a hit every 90 seconds. Your nervous system was built for hits every 90 minutes. The math is the entire problem. The math is also the entire cure."*
End text: **90 seconds without it. start there.**

---

## V5 — Shiva voice, 12s
Pure silence for first 7s. Then one line:
*"The one who is tired — is not you."*
End text: **rest there.**

## V6 — Krishna, 35s
*"Arjuna stood on a battlefield with the most important decision of his life and could not move. I did not tell him to take a day off. I did not tell him to journal. I told him: do the work. drop the result. 5,000 years later, every productivity book is still trying to say this and missing."*
End text: **act. release.**

---

## V7 — Buddha, 22s

End text: **see clearly. the rest follows.**

## V9 — Ramana, 15s

## V10 — Patanjali, 20s

## V11 — Shiva, 30s
*"You have been chasing a version of yourself you saw in someone else's eyes. Stop. Sit. The version of you that lives underneath the chasing has been waiting on this mountain for 10,000 years. It will wait one more day. But today is enough."*
End text: **come home.**

## V12 — Krishna, 18s
*"Your work is your work. The reward is not yours. The praise is not yours. The result is not yours. Only the work — done with full attention — is yours. Everything else is borrowed."*

## V13 — Buddha, 28s
*"The first noble truth is dukkha — unsatisfactoriness. You scroll because nothing satisfies. You shop because nothing satisfies. You achieve because nothing satisfies. The cure is not to find the satisfying thing. The cure is to stop running from the dissatisfaction."*
End text: **stay with it. it dissolves.**

## V14 — Modern Guru, 22s

End text: **for you. at 11:47 PM.**
