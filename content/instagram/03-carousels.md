## C21 — "The Witness practice (try it in 60 seconds)"
1. Sit. Eyes open or closed. Doesn't matter.
2. Notice the next thought that arises.
3. Don't follow it. Don't fight it.
4. Note: *thought*. Then return to breath.
5. The thought will return. Note: *thought*. Return.
6. After 60 seconds, count how many thoughts arrived.
7. (For most people: 18–24.)
8. The fact that you counted means you were the witness.
9. The witness is the practice.
10. **Save this.** Do it now. The whole game is in 60 seconds. — *Vatsal*
