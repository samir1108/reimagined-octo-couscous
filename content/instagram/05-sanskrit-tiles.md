# Instagram — Sanskrit Tiles (10 single-image posts)

Format per tile:
- **Top third:** verse in Devanagari (large, gold)
- **Middle:** transliteration (italic, smaller)
- **Lower third:** English translation (Cormorant)
- **Footer:** source citation
- Background: black with subtle bhasma-ash texture

---

## S1 — Yoga Sutras 1.2
**योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः ।**
*yogaḥ citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ*
**Yoga is the cessation of the fluctuations of the mind.**
— Patanjali, Yoga Sutras 1.2

---

## S2 — Chandogya Upanishad 6.8.7
**तत्त्वमसि ।**
*tat tvam asi*
**Thou art that.**
— Chandogya Upanishad 6.8.7

---

## S3 — Bhagavad Gita 2.47
**कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।**
*karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana*
**You have a right to the action — never to the fruits of action.**
— Bhagavad Gita 2.47

---

## S4 — Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.4.10
**अहं ब्रह्मास्मि ।**
*aham brahmāsmi*
**I am the absolute.**
— Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.4.10

---

## S5 — Mandukya Upanishad
**सोऽहम् ।**
*so'ham*
**I am that.** (also the natural sound of the breath)
— Mandukya Upanishad

---

## S6 — Yoga Sutras 1.33
**मैत्रीकरुणामुदितोपेक्षाणाम् ।**
*maitrī-karuṇā-muditā-upekṣāṇām*
**Friendliness, compassion, joy, equanimity — toward all beings.**
— Patanjali, Yoga Sutras 1.33

---

## S7 — Ashtavakra Gita 1.4
**मुक्तिमिच्छसि चेत्तात विषयान्विषवत्त्यज ।**
*muktim icchasi cet tāta viṣayān viṣavat tyaja*
**If you wish to be free, dear one — let go of the senses as you would let go of poison.**
— Ashtavakra Gita 1.4

---

## S8 — Vigyan Bhairav Tantra (technique)
**श्वासे श्वासे विश्रामम् ।**
*śvāse śvāse viśrāmam*
**Rest in each breath.**
— Vigyan Bhairav Tantra (paraphrased)

---

## S9 — Bhagavad Gita 6.5
**उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम् ।**
*uddhared ātmanātmānam*
**Lift yourself by yourself.**
— Bhagavad Gita 6.5

---

## S10 — Isha Upanishad
**ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदम् ।**
*oṃ pūrṇam adaḥ pūrṇam idam*
**That is whole. This is whole. From wholeness, wholeness emerges.**
— Isha Upanishad (invocation)
