# Instagram — App Cinematics (5 product reels × 30s)

Goal: make the app itself the aesthetic. Real screens floating in cinematic space. No "tutorial energy." Treat the product like a Pixar character.

---

## A1 — "Opening the Temple" (30s)
- **0–5s** Black. A finger taps the home screen icon. Slow zoom into the gold flame as it opens.
- **5–15s** Camera pulls into the Temple Hall: 3D Padmasana masters seated in a circle, dim ambient gold light.
- **15–22s** A single master (Krishna) lifts his eyes. Camera POV. Text: *"the system picks the guru you need today."*
- **22–28s** UI element appears: *"today: a single conversation."*
- **28–30s** Flame logo. *"the temple is open."*

---

## A2 — "The Witness Practice" (30s)
- **0–5s** Camera follows a real user opening the Witness screen.
- **5–15s** UI: a thought counter ticking up — 1, 2, 3, 7, 14 — as gold orbs drift past.
- **15–22s** VO (Modern Guru): *"don't fight the thought. note it. return to the breath."*
- **22–28s** The counter softly fades. The screen settles. Heart rate display drops.
- **28–30s** Text: *"the witness is the practice."*

---

## A3 — "Loop Detected" (30s)
- **0–5s** Phone notification arrives at 11:47 PM. *"reactivity spike detected. one breath?"*
- **5–15s** User taps. The screen darkens. A 4-6 breath orb appears. They follow.
- **15–22s** UI overlay: *"VAL003 — reactivity spike. avg duration: 47 min. interrupted: 90 sec."*
- **22–28s** The phone goes face-down. Screen black.
- **28–30s** *"the loop loses 80% of its power the moment it is named."*

---

## A4 — "The 4 Gates" (30s)
- **0–6s** Sunrise → 6 AM gate animates open in app.
- **6–12s** Noon → midday witness pulse appears.
- **12–18s** Sunset → evening integration screen.
- **18–24s** Moon → night release screen, soft yoga nidra visual.
- **24–30s** All four gates orbit a single still figure. *"every wisdom tradition mapped these. we shipped them as software."*

---

## A5 — "The Council of Yogis" (30s)
- **0–8s** Animated 13 gold figures in a circle. Slow rotation.
- **8–18s** UI overlay: real-time inputs streaming in — current loop, time of day, last 7 sessions, body rhythm.
- **18–24s** One figure illuminates. The system has chosen.
- **24–30s** Cut to user opening the app. Krishna appears. *"the system sees what you can't."*
