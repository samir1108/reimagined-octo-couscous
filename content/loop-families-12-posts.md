# 12 Posts — The 12 Loop Families
*Me trying to understand myself. One post per family.*

---

## Post 1 — Validation Loops

First thing I do is check notifications. Then I send a message and look at it 5 times to see if it’s been seen. No likes? Mood dips a little.

This is VAL001–003. The dopamine circuit works like a slot machine — Lembke writes about this in Dopamine Nation. The reward might come, might not, might come soon, might not. That uncertainty itself is the drug.

Where it comes from: childhood where love was conditional on performance.

How to get out: do one good thing and tell no one. Do it again tomorrow.

Paired path: Karma (anonymous service), Bhakti (inner Beloved).

This loop needs an audience. Starve it quietly.

— Vatsal

## Post 2 — Avoidance Loops

That task that’s hopped across 4 to-do lists. Opening 8 tabs to avoid doing the one thing that actually matters.

This is AVO001–002. The anterior cingulate detects effort cost, and the prefrontal just goes for immediate relief. Procrastination isn’t laziness — it’s emotional regulation. The nervous system isn’t broken, it’s just trying to protect you because it thinks the task is a threat.

Where it comes from: when task = punishment, from past experiences of failing something similar.

How to get out: 2-minute rule. Open the document. Don’t write. Just open it. Daily.

Paired path: Raja (yama: tapas), Karma (skill in action).

The first step is always the hardest. Make it so small you can’t say no.

— Vatsal

## Post 3 — Control Loops

Rewriting that email 12 times. Micromanaging other people. Body tightens before any uncertainty shows up.

This is CTL001–002. The amygdala is chronically activated, the prefrontal is over-modulating to compensate. Anxiety = control without information. The body thinks unpredictability is unsafe because it once was — and it’s trying to manage what can’t actually be managed.

Where it comes from: early environment where nothing was predictable, and control became the only safety the body knew.

How to get out: hand over one decision per week to someone else. Don’t review it.

Paired path: Bhakti (surrender), Tantra (yes to what is).

You’re not the manager of the universe. Practice letting someone else run it for 24 hours.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 4 — Performance Loops

Always ‘on’. Drained by small talk. Even during rest, brain’s planning the next thing.

This is PER001–002. The self-evaluative cortex (mPFC) is always engaged; the default mode network never fully shuts off during rest. The nervous system has no off-switch because it was never given permission to have one. Your identity became a product, and products have to keep shipping.

Where it comes from: love = output. Worth = production.

How to get out: 10 unproductive minutes daily, scheduled. Stare. Wander. Don’t justify it.

Paired path: Karma (fruit-renunciation), Tantra (presence over output).

You don’t have to produce anything. Your worth doesn’t run on a quarterly target.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 5 — Comparison Loops

Other people’s lives look sharper than yours. Scrolling leaves a subtle ache behind.

This is CMP001–002. Social comparison activates the same neural circuits as physical pain (dACC). Instagram is basically a pain machine. You’re not imagining the ache — it’s structurally similar to a cut, a burn, a bruise. The platform is designed to exploit that circuit, and that circuit is designed to make you want to be someone else.

Where it comes from: sibling/peer ranking, school cultures of relative worth.

How to get out: 30-day fast from one comparison surface. Notice what fills the silence.

Paired path: Jnana (who is comparing?), Bhakti (longing redirected).

When you stop looking at other people’s lives, you get yours back.

— Vatsal

## Post 6 — Rumination Loops

That same conversation, replayed at 11:47 PM, with better comebacks every single time.

This is RUM001–002. Default mode network is overactive, the PFC is stuck in narrative simulation. The brain thinks it’s solving the problem. It’s not — it’s rehearsing the wound. The body can’t resolve thought using thought. It has to resolve it through sensation.

Where it comes from: unprocessed emotion looking for resolution through thinking (and it’s never gonna find it there).

How to get out: body-first. When rumination starts, walk 10 minutes. Don’t think. Just walk.

Paired path: Kriya (body-first), Raja (pratyahara).

The mind is a storyteller, not a healer. Ask your body for the answer.

— Vatsal

## Post 7 — Spiritual Bypass Loops

Quoting Rumi instead of feeling. Saying ‘it’s all one’ when something specific is actually hurting.

This is SPB001–002. Cognitive reframing without limbic processing — the concept overrides the feeling, but the feeling stays in the body. The teaching is true. The bypass isn’t. You’re using wisdom to avoid the very thing wisdom was meant to transform.

Where it comes from: pain arrived before the tools to actually feel it.

How to get out: when you reach for a teaching, ask yourself — what am I trying not to feel?

Paired path: Tantra (full embrace), Bhakti (sit with the ache).

If your spirituality has no heat in it, it’s not working. It’s hiding.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 8 — Scarcity Loops

Money, time, love — never enough. Hoarding. Bracing for the next loss.

This is SCR001–002. Cognitive bandwidth narrows under perceived scarcity (Mullainathan & Shafir, Scarcity, 2013). The nervous system doesn’t process absence — it processes the *anticipation* of absence. The body lives in a famine that hasn’t even happened yet, and defends every resource like it’s the last one left.

Where it comes from: early lack — material or emotional — encoded in the nervous system.

How to get out: daily, name 3 things that are present. Specific. Concrete. Out loud.

Paired path: Karma (trust through doing), Bhakti (faith).

The body believes what you tell it — when you tell it enough times, specifically enough, and loud enough.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 9 — Savior Loops

Always the helper. Tired but can’t say no. Identity built entirely on being needed.

This is SAV001. Caretaking activates reward circuits; saying no triggers shame circuits. You’re not kind. You’re addicted to the debt people owe you. That debt is the only thing you believe keeps them close.

Where it comes from: parentified child; love earned through usefulness.

How to get out: say no once this week, no explanation. Just survive it.

Paired path: Karma (real service has limits), Jnana (who needs to be needed?).

Your value isn’t in your utility. The people who actually love you will survive your no.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 10 — Victim Loops

Life happens to you. Same patterns, again and again. The story of being wronged is so rehearsed it feels like truth.

This is VCT001. Locus of control is externalized; cortisol patterns look like learned helplessness (Seligman). The body has taken on an identity that needs an enemy. The enemy is real. So was the helplessness. But the body chose to stay in the shape of the wound long after the weapon was put down.

Where it comes from: real powerlessness, often early, that crystallized into identity.

How to get out: daily, one small thing fully chosen — not endured.

Paired path: Karma (action), Raja (yama).

The first 10 seconds of a chosen action have more healing power than 10 years of having been wronged.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 11 — Addictive Loops

Substance, scroll, sugar, sex — the thing that promises peace and delivers more wanting.

This is ADX001. Dopamine deficit state — the activity no longer brings pleasure, only relief from absence. The body learned: discomfort can only be escaped, not felt. This isn’t a moral failure. It’s a nervous system that started running before it ever got to finish feeling what it was leaving behind.

Where it comes from: nervous system that never learned — discomfort can be felt, not fled.

How to get out: 30-day fast from that one thing. Feel what shows up. That’s the actual practice.

Paired path: Raja (full path), Kriya (body re-regulation).

The craving isn’t a failure of will. It’s a body trying to be heard, not silenced.

— Vatsal

---

## Post 12 — Perfectionism Loops

Nothing ships. The standard shifts every time you get close. Self-criticism dressed up as high standards.

This is PFX001. The internalized critic (lateral PFC) is hyperactive; ventromedial reward is muted. Every completion gets devalued because it was never good enough to earn the approval that was promised. This loop isn’t about excellence. It’s about a kid who learned that love came after the product, never before.

Where it comes from: love that came after performance, never before.

How to get out: ship one thing this week at 70%. Watch what does and doesn’t happen.

Paired path: Karma (skill, not perfection), Tantra (include the flaw).

The world doesn’t need your perfect anything. It needs your real something.

— Vatsal
