## THE FULL CATALOG — 56 games

### 🎯 ATTENTION & PERCEPTION (Dharana)

**1. Bell in the Wind** — Distant bell in a void. Don't look away. Tab-blur, scroll, cursor-leave = miss. *(Yoga Sutra 3.1, single-pointed concentration. Mechanism: sustained-attention network, dlPFC.)*

**2. Candle in the Storm** — Hold the device steady; tilt jitter (DeviceMotion API) blows out the flame. *(Trataka, candle-gazing. Mechanism: oculomotor + postural attention coupling.)*

**3. Two Hands** — Tap left/right in alternating rhythm. Measures bilateral coordination & cross-hemispheric attention. *(EMDR / cross-crawl somatics. Mechanism: corpus-callosum traffic.)*

**4. Floor Beneath the Floor** — Change-blindness. Subtle scene shifts you must catch. *(Vipassanā micro-noticing. Mechanism: top-down vs. bottom-up attention, Simons & Chabris.)*

**5. Listen for the Bird** — A bird call hides in 60s of ambient. Tap the instant you hear it. *(Shabd yoga. Mechanism: auditory attention latency.)*

### 🌬️ BODY & NERVOUS SYSTEM

**6. Breath Temple** ⭐ — Mic-detected breath blooms a lotus, locked to 5.5 bpm coherence. *(VBT §24 + HeartMath. Mechanism: vagal afferent at resonance freq.)*

**7. Cold Water** — Splash cold water on your face. Front-camera brightness delta + mic gasp detection. *(Diving reflex. Mechanism: trigeminal-vagal arc, Hayashi 2003.)*

**8. The Sigh** — Mic detects spontaneous 2-phase sighs. *(Cyclic sighing — Balban/Huberman 2023, Cell Reports Medicine. Mechanism: alveolar reset + parasympathetic pulse.)*

**9. Heart Coherence Bridge** — *Reimagined without HRV hardware.* Mic-breath at 5.5 bpm builds a bridge of light. After coherence, AI-generated letter from past-you. *(McCraty 2009.)*

**10. The Yawn Loop** — Tap when you *feel* a yawn coming, not when it happens. *(Interoceptive prediction. Mechanism: insula precision-weighting, Critchley.)*

**11. Body Scanner** — 12 zones. Tap *only* where sensation is real. Tap entropy + dwell = interoceptive accuracy. *(Goenka body sweep.)*

**53. Breath Ladder** — Guided 4-7-8 with mic verification of actual exhale length. *(Andrew Weil / Pranayama. Mechanism: parasympathetic dominance at 1:2 inhale:exhale.)*

### 👁️ WITNESS & OBSERVATION (Sakshi Bhāv)

**12. Thought Watcher** — Words drift up. Tap = "I saw it." Over-tap (false positive) and under-tap (miss) both fail. Hidden sensation-words give 3× score. *(Vipassanā noting, Goldstein.)*

**13. Sound Microscope** — Drone with sub-tones shifting 1Hz/30s, then below JND threshold. Tap on shift. *(Nada yoga. Mechanism: tonotopic acuity.)*

**14. Two Mirrors** — Webcam gaze stability. Look at yourself without looking away. Eye-tracking via face-api.js. *(Ramakrishna mirror sadhana.)*

**15. The 30-Second Mind** — Self-report mind-wandering. Calibrates honesty over sessions (consistency score). *(MBSR weekly inventory.)*

**36. The Witness Wall** — 60s of nothing. Measures urge-to-do via micro-touches and DeviceMotion accelerometer noise. *(Shikantaza.)*

**39. The Witness Room** — Sound-reactive. Random environmental audio plays mid-meditation. Score = unreactive presence *during* stimulus. *(Already shipping in Temple as Witness practice.)*

**46. The Empty Room** — No UI at all. Black screen. Ends when you say "enough" (mic). Measures how long you can tolerate nothing. *(Patanjali nirodha.)*

**17. The Scroll** ⭐ — Fake generative social feed (Groq-generated). Composite stress signal: scroll velocity, dwell, mic, typing. AI verdict at end. *(Mere-measurement effect.)*

**18. The Compliment Trap** — Choice latency on praise vs. neutral. Reveals which compliments hijack you. *(Ego inflation, Trungpa "spiritual materialism".)*

**44. The Coherent Three** *(NEW)* — All 3 brains must agree before action. Each shows a slider (head: logic 0-10, heart: warmth 0-10, gut: certainty 0-10). When all >7 you can proceed. Trains decision-coherence. *(Polyvagal + cardiac-vagal synchrony.)*
