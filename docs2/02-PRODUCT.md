## 1. Product Philosophy

### 1.1 Design Principles

ConsciousOS follows seven core design principles that govern every product decision:

**Principle 1: Depth Over Distraction**
Every screen, every interaction, every animation serves the user's consciousness journey. No decorative elements exist purely for aesthetic pleasure. Visual design creates space for awareness rather than demanding attention.

**Principle 2: Adaptive Intelligence**
The app never asks the user to adapt to it. Instead, it adapts to the user — their time, energy, mood, developmental level, communication style, and life circumstances. If a user has 2 minutes, the app serves a 2-minute experience. If they have 45 minutes, it fills that time meaningfully.

**Principle 3: Gentle Authority**
The AI companion guides without commanding. It suggests without demanding. It observes without judging. The user always maintains autonomy. At no point does the app create guilt, urgency, or obligation. Missing a day is acknowledged with warmth, not with streak-break penalties.

**Principle 4: Progressive Revelation**
Features and depth reveal themselves as the user is ready. A day-1 user sees a simpler interface than a day-30 user. Advanced features don't overwhelm beginners, and basic features don't bore advanced practitioners.

**Principle 5: Cinematic Presence**
The visual and auditory experience creates a sense of entering a sacred space. Animations are deliberate, transitions are smooth, and the overall aesthetic communicates reverence for the inner journey.

**Principle 6: Multi-Tradition Respect**
No single wisdom tradition is presented as superior. The app draws from multiple streams and presents wisdom in universal, accessible language while honoring the depth and specificity of each source tradition.

**Principle 7: Designed Obsolescence**
The product's ultimate success metric is users who no longer need it. Every feature is designed to build the user's internal resources rather than create dependency on external tools.

### 1.2 Visual Design Language

ConsciousOS employs a "spiritual-tech" design language:

- **Glassmorphism**: Frosted glass effects create depth and layering, suggesting the transparent nature of consciousness itself
- **Ambient Backgrounds**: Subtle, time-of-day responsive particle systems and gradients that create atmosphere without distraction
- **Breathing Animations**: Circular, rhythmic animations that unconsciously entrain the user's breathing toward calm
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (display) + DM Sans (body) — clean, modern, and readable
- **Color Themes**: Five premium themes (Obsidian, Abyss, Sakura, Terra, Nebula) with full dark/light mode support
- **Iconography**: Lucide icons for consistent, recognizable UI elements

## 2. User Journey: First 30 Days

### 2.1 Day 0: Discovery and Signup

The user's first encounter with ConsciousOS is carefully crafted:

**Cinematic Intro**: A 5-second animation introduces the brand — the infinity symbol pulses with golden light, establishing the visual language. This plays once per browser session.

**Landing Page**: A long-form narrative page that communicates:
- The problem (living on autopilot)
- The solution (AI-powered consciousness companion)
- How it works (daily adaptive practices)
- The philosophy (observe, inquire, release)
- Social proof (testimonials)
- Pricing and features
- Enterprise offering

**Authentication**: Clean, minimal auth screen with email/password signup. Email verification required before first access.

### 2.2 Day 0: Onboarding

After email verification, new users enter the onboarding flow:

**Step 1: Name & Identity**
- Display name (used by the Guru in all communications)
- Optional avatar

**Step 2: Initial Assessment**
- Current emotional state (5-point mood scale)
- Energy level
- Primary reason for using ConsciousOS
- Previous meditation/mindfulness experience

**Step 3: Theme Selection**
- Users choose their visual theme from five options
- Each theme is presented with its character (e.g., "Obsidian — Deep black + liquid gold")

**Step 4: Language & Communication**
- Preferred language (currently English + Hindi)
- Communication style preference (gentle, direct, poetic)

**Step 5: Life Domains**
- Users select 3-5 primary life domains they want to focus on
- This seeds the initial consciousness profile

### 2.3 Days 1-7: The Foundation Week

The first week is critical for habit formation and is carefully designed:

**Morning Insights (1 per day)**: Each day delivers a specific insight designed to build fundamental understanding:

| Day | Insight | Purpose |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | "Today you begin to see the mind for what it is — a tool, not you." | Introducing witness consciousness |
| 2 | "If your mind resisted opening this app, that's the pattern we're here to observe." | Normalizing resistance |
| 3 | "If meditation feels useless, that's normal. Your brain is resisting change." | Managing expectations |
| 4 | "Your brain saves energy by running on autopilot." | Understanding the mechanism |
| 5 | "Notice today: sensations arise in the body, but who is watching them?" | First inquiry pointer |
| 6 | "Knowing something intellectually and living it are different worlds." | Bridging theory and practice |
| 7 | "You chose to open this app despite a world designed to keep you distracted." | Celebrating commitment |

**Evening Insights**: Complementary evening reflections that help process the day's experiences through the lens of the morning insight.

**Gentle Engagement**: Notifications are warm and non-urgent during week 1. The system seeds 3-5 notifications during onboarding covering:
- Journal introduction
- Guide introduction
- Practice introduction
- Streak encouragement
- Community welcome

### 2.4 Days 8-14: The Deepening Week

Week 2 introduces deeper concepts:
- Core Loops begin to be identified
- Pattern recognition becomes a daily practice
- The AI companion starts referencing previous conversations
- Shadow work is gently introduced
- The user starts to see connections between days

**Critical Retention Moment**: Week 2 is statistically the highest dropout period for wellness apps. ConsciousOS addresses this with:
- "Week 2 Anchor" messages: "🌱 Most people stop here. You're still here. That matters."
- Reduced practice demands (shorter sessions available)
- More celebration of small wins
- The AI companion acknowledges the difficulty explicitly

### 2.5 Days 15-21: The Integration Week

Week 3 focuses on bringing awareness into daily life:
- Observation tasks become more sophisticated
- The gap between trigger and reaction is explicitly tracked
- Body awareness deepens
- Dreams become a regular part of the conversation
- The user's consciousness score begins to show meaningful movement

### 2.6 Days 22-30: The Transformation Week

The final week of the initial journey:
- Self-inquiry questions deepen
- The witness identity begins to stabilize
- Integration across life domains becomes visible
- The user is invited to look back at Day 1 and notice the change
- Day 30 marks the completion of "initial awakening" — the foundation is laid

### 2.7 Day 30+: The Ongoing Journey

After day 30, the app shifts from structured onboarding to full adaptive mode:
- Morning insights become contextual rather than sequential
- Programs become available for focused development
- Community features open up
- The AI companion draws from a richer memory base
- Advanced features and practices unlock

## 3. The Daily Cycle: A Complete Walkthrough

### 3.1 State Machine Architecture

The daily cycle is managed by two XState state machines:

**dailyCycleMachine**: Manages the overall daily flow
- States: `morning_gate`, `midday_pulse`, `evening_sandhya`, `night_sanctuary`
- Transitions based on time-of-day and completed activities
- Context tracks: morning completion, evening completion, streak, task counts

**homeMachine**: Manages navigation and interaction
- States: `idle`, `selecting_slot`, `navigating`
- Handles slot selection, navigation triggers

### 3.2 Time Slot Configuration

```
Morning:   5:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Midday:    12:00 PM - 6:00 PM
Evening:   6:00 PM - 10:00 PM
Night:     10:00 PM - 5:00 AM
```

Each time slot has:
- A greeting ("Good morning", "Good afternoon", etc.)
- An emoji (🌅, ☀️, 🌆, 🌙)
- A color scheme
- Specific available features and practices

### 3.3 Session System

Sessions represent focused engagement periods:
- Each time slot can have one session
- Sessions have statuses: `active`, `completed`
- Completing a session awards XP and updates consciousness metrics
- Sessions are stored in the database with timestamps, type, and summary

## 4. Morning Gate: Deep Dive

### 4.1 Entry Experience

When the user opens the app in the morning, they encounter the Morning Gate — a full-screen, contemplative entry experience.

**Visual Elements:**
- A breathing circle animation (4-7-8 pattern) — a gentle visual pulse that unconsciously guides breathing
- The user's current band icon (representing their V-Level)
- A warm greeting with their name
- Their journey day counter
- A morning insight (days 1-30, contextual thereafter)

**The Breathing Circle**: This is not decorative. The animation follows a modified 4-7-8 breathing pattern:
- 4 seconds: Circle expands (inhale)
- 7 seconds: Circle holds (hold)
- 8 seconds: Circle contracts (exhale)
- Duration: 19 seconds per cycle

This creates a 15-30 second contemplative space before any action is required.

### 4.2 Dream Capture

Before the morning session begins, users are offered a prominent Dream Capture section:

**Two Options:**
1. **Write in Journal**: Opens the journal with a pre-filled dream capture prompt
2. **Talk about your Dream**: Opens the Guide in dream_analysis mode

**Why Dreams Matter**: Dreams are processed by the AI as consciousness data — recurring themes, emotional patterns, and symbolic content are correlated with the user's waking consciousness patterns. Over time, the system can identify pre-breakthrough dream patterns and regression indicators.

### 4.3 Morning Session Flow

When the user taps "Begin Morning Session," they enter a guided session:

1. **Opening**: The Guide greets them with awareness of yesterday's events
2. **Check-in**: Mood, energy, available time
3. **Intention Setting**: Based on the day's theme
4. **Practice**: Guided awareness exercise appropriate to their level
5. **Closing**: Summary and preview of the day

### 4.4 Streak Display

The streak is displayed in the top-right corner:
- **Fire emoji** with consecutive day count
- **Golden glow** at 30+ days
- **Candle emoji** during grace period (missed 1 day)
- Gentle pulse animation proportional to streak length

## 7. Night Sanctuary: Deep Dive

### 7.1 Design Philosophy

Night mode is the most minimal and contemplative of all gates. The design principle: **less is more**. The screen is darker, animations are slower, and text is sparser. The goal is to facilitate the transition from conscious activity to restful integration.

### 7.2 Components

**Breathing Orb**: A large, slow-breathing circle (8-second cycle) centered on screen, with a moon icon. This creates an immediate calming effect.

**Phase Dots Visualization**: Shows the day's completed gates as a horizontal progress:
- Morning ✅ or 🌅
- Midday ☀️ or ✅
- Evening 🌆 or ✅
- Night 🌙 (always current)

**Night Message**: Contextual based on the day's trajectory:
- After a dark night: "You showed up today. That's courage. Rest gently."
- After a breakthrough: "Something beautiful is unfolding. Let sleep deepen it."
- After a quiet day: "Today was quiet. That's okay. Tomorrow is a new dawn."
- Default: "Rest. Integrate. Grow."

**Dream Seed**: A pre-sleep contemplation linked to the user's current focus dimension:
- Body focus: "Tonight, let your body speak to you in dreams."
- Emotion focus: "As you drift off, notice what emotions are gently present..."
- Thought focus: "Let your mind wander freely tonight."
- Awareness focus: "Notice the moment between waking and sleep..."

**Three Gratitudes**: An inline journaling feature for capturing three things to be grateful for. Saved to journal with "gratitude" and "night" tags.

**Night Suggestions**: 2-4 contextual suggestions combining journal and guide options:
- Evening Reflection (journal)
- Emotional Support / Shadow Work / Gratitude & Joy (guide modes)
- Letter to Yourself (journal, during difficult periods)

### 7.3 Quiet Day Handling

If the user had no engagement during the day (no sessions, no tasks), the Night Sanctuary acknowledges this without judgment:
- Phase dots show no completions
- Message: "Today was quiet. That's okay. Tomorrow is a new dawn."
- Gratitudes and journal are still available
- No missed session guilt

## 8. The Guide: AI Companion Experience

### 8.1 Interface Design

The Guide occupies a dedicated page (`/app/guide`) with a chat-style interface:

**Chat Area:**
- Messages displayed in conversation bubbles
- User messages aligned right
- Guru messages aligned left with subtle avatar
- Markdown support for formatted responses
- Auto-scroll to latest message
- Loading indicator during response generation

**Input Area:**
- Text input with send button
- Voice input button (ElevenLabs integration)
- Mode selector (when entering from specific context)

### 8.2 Guide Modes

Users can enter the Guide with specific modes:

**General** (default): Open conversation about whatever the user needs
**Therapy**: Deeper emotional processing with therapeutic techniques
**Shadow Work**: Exploring hidden aspects of self, triggers, projections
**Dream Analysis**: Interpreting and exploring dream content
**Somatic**: Body-focused awareness and processing
**Breathwork**: Guided breathing practices
**Gratitude**: Appreciation and joy cultivation
**Inquiry**: Deep self-investigation (Who am I?)
**Loop Work**: Specific loop identification and dissolution

Each mode adjusts the AI's system prompt, communication style, and suggested practices.

### 8.3 Voice Interaction

**Speech-to-Text (STT):**
- Powered by ElevenLabs Scribe
- Real-time transcription during voice input
- Supports multiple languages

**Text-to-Speech (TTS):**
- Powered by ElevenLabs
- Natural-sounding voice that matches the Guru's personality
- Can read responses aloud for an immersive experience

### 8.4 Guide Memory

Every Guide conversation:
1. Creates session messages stored in the database
2. Triggers memory extraction (key insights, patterns, emotions)
3. Updates the user's consciousness profile
4. Feeds into the consciousness engine for future personalization

### 8.5 Context-Aware Entry

The Guide can be entered with pre-loaded context:
- From Morning Gate: morning check-in mode with today's theme
- From Dream Capture: dream_analysis mode
- From Evening Sandhya: evening reflection mode with day summary
- From Presence Card: awareness exploration with specific prompt
- From AI Suggestions: specific mode with contextual setup

## 10. Practice Engine

### 10.1 Task Types

The practice engine manages three types of consciousness tasks:

**Daily Tasks**: AI-generated tasks based on the user's current consciousness profile
- Generated by the consciousness engine each morning
- Aligned with the day's theme and focus dimension
- Varied in difficulty and domain

**Observation Tasks**: Micro-awareness exercises for daily life
- Chain-able (completing one unlocks the next)
- Focused on specific consciousness skills
- Include reflection prompts after completion
- Categorized by domain (body, emotion, thought, awareness)

**Program Tasks**: Structured tasks from enrolled programs
- Follow the program's daily curriculum
- Include knowledge reading, practice, and reflection components

### 10.2 Task Completion Flow

When a user completes a task:
1. Task status updates to "completed" with timestamp
2. The system triggers the `complete-task` edge function
3. The edge function:
   - Logs the completion
   - Updates consciousness metrics
   - Generates a chain task (if applicable)
   - Extracts consciousness signals from any reflection
4. The home dashboard updates to reflect the new state

### 10.3 Observation Meditation

The Practice page (`/app/practice`) includes a dedicated Observation Meditation feature:
- Guided meditation with ambient visuals
- Rooted in Witness Consciousness tradition
- Progressive depth based on V-Level
- Timer with gentle bell notifications

## 12. Analytics & Consciousness Score

| Category | Max Points | Inputs |
|----------|-----------|--------|

| Consistency | 200 | Streak, total sessions, journal entries |
| Witness | 200 | Witness depth, stability, gap |
| Integration | 200 | Integration score from profile |
| Loop Mastery | 100 | Loop awareness minus bypassing risk |

**Score Tiers:**
| Range | Tier | Character |
|-------|------|-----------|
| 0-150 | Dormant | Beginning of the journey |
| 151-300 | Stirring | First signs of awakening |
| 301-500 | Awakening | Growing awareness |
| 501-700 | Expanding | Sustained development |
| 701-850 | Radiant | Deep transformation |

**Trend Indicators:**

## 13. Programs: Structured Journeys

### 13.1 Program Structure

Each program consists of:

```
Program
├── Metadata (title, description, duration, category, difficulty)
├── Day 1
│   ├── Knowledge (educational content)
│   ├── Practice (awareness exercise)
│   └── Reflection (journaling prompt)
├── Day 2
│   ├── Knowledge
│   ├── Practice
│   └── Reflection
...
└── Day N
    ├── Knowledge
    ├── Practice
    └── Reflection
```

### 13.2 Enrollment System

- Users can browse programs in the Explore section
- Enrolling in a program creates a `program_enrollment` record
- Only one program can be active at a time
- Daily progress is tracked at the day level:
  - `knowledge_read`: Boolean
  - `practice_done`: Boolean
  - `reflection_text`: Optional text
  - `completed_at`: Timestamp when all three are done

### 13.3 Grace Days

Programs include grace days to prevent dropout from perfectionism:
- Missing a day doesn't break the program
- Grace days used are tracked
- After exceeding grace days, the program pauses (not cancels)
- Programs can be resumed from the pause point

### 13.4 Available Programs

**Emotional Track:**

**Additional Programs (stubs for future development):**

## 16. Onboarding Flow

1. `checkingAuth` → Check for existing session

## 17. Edge Cases & Graceful Degradation

### 17.1 Missed Morning

When a user arrives after the morning window:

| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Arrived at midday, morning slot open | Show Condensed Morning (quick version) |
| Arrived in evening, missed everything | Show Late Bloomer mode (no guilt, fresh start) |
| Arrived at night | Show Night Sanctuary with quiet day acknowledgment |
| Returned after 3+ days | Show Welcome Back (mood check, gentle re-entry) |

### 17.2 No Theme Generated

If the consciousness engine fails to generate a daily theme:
- Fallback to a generic but contextual theme
- Error logged but user experience uninterrupted
- Retry on next check-in

### 17.3 No Internet Connection

- React Query persistence via IndexedDB

- Skeleton shimmer loading states
