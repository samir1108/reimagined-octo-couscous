### 2.2 Revenue Model: Credit Economy

```
THE CREDIT ECONOMY:

Core Principle: Users buy credits and spend them on AI interactions.
This aligns revenue with value delivered (more AI usage = more value = more revenue).

How Credits Work:
├── Users purchase credit packs (₹99, ₹299, ₹999, ₹4,999)
├── Platform takes 25% margin (₹100 purchase = ₹75 usable credits)
├── Credits are consumed by AI interactions:
│   ├── Guide chat message: ~₹0.50-₹2.00 per exchange
│   ├── Journal reflection: ~₹1.00-₹3.00 per analysis
│   ├── Consciousness engine update: ~₹0.50-₹1.50 per computation
│   ├── Memory consolidation: ~₹0.20-₹0.50 per cycle
│   └── Voice interaction: ~₹1.00-₹5.00 per session
│
├── Free tier: 50 credits/month (enough for basic exploration)
├── Power users: Typically spend ₹299-₹999/month
└── Enterprise: Bulk credit allocation per employee

Why Credit Model vs Subscription:
├── ✅ Aligns revenue with actual value delivered
├── ✅ No "paying for something I don't use" resentment
├── ✅ Users control spending granularly
├── ✅ Lower barrier to start (buy small, grow naturally)
├── ✅ Higher potential ARPU than flat subscription
├── ✅ Encourages deeper engagement (more use = more purchase)
├── ❌ Less predictable revenue than subscription
├── ❌ Requires careful credit pricing to maintain margin
└── ❌ Users may ration credits (friction in experience)

Mitigation for downsides:
├── Auto-reload option for seamless experience
├── Monthly credit packs for subscription-like behavior
├── Free tier ensures basic engagement without credits
└── Enterprise contracts provide revenue predictability
```
