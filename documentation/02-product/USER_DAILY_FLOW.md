# ConsciousOS: User Daily Flow Guide

## Overview

ConsciousOS offers a **radically personalized daily journey experience** powered by the Adaptive System. The Adaptive System generates **unique daily packages** based on your:

- Current V-Level (invisible vertical consciousness development)
- H-Levels (12 horizontal capacities for conscious living)
- 9 Life Domains (where patterns show up)
- 7 Active Loops (unconscious patterns)
- Real-time emotional/energy state

This guide outlines the typical daily flow, helping you understand what to expect each day as the AI-powered Adaptive System adapts to your unique journey.

---

## 1. Morning: Starting Your Day

### 1.1 Wake Up & Initial Check-In (5am-12pm)

#### For All Users
- **Night Mode Transition**: If you wake up between 5am-9am, the app transitions from night mode to morning
- **Dream Journal Prompt**: Appears if you haven't recorded a dream yet (optional but recommended)

#### Morning Session with AI
- **AI Conversation**: You begin your day with a personalized AI conversation. The AI engages with you to understand:
  - How you're feeling (mood)
  - Your current energy level
  - How much time you have available today
  - Your intention for the day
- **Adaptive Check-In**: The AI adapts its questions based on your responses, creating a natural conversational flow
- **Day Package Generation**: Based on the AI conversation, the system creates a personalized set of blocks tailored to your:
  - Current V-Level and H-Levels
  - Active loops and patterns
  - Available time and energy
  - Personal intentions

---

## 2. All Day: Engaging with Your Journey

### 2.1 Day Package Block Execution

- **Block Types**: Practice, Learn, Reflect, Inquiry, Embodiment, Shadow Work
- **Flexible Order**: Complete blocks in any order that fits your schedule
- **Capacity Focus**: Blocks designed to strengthen specific H-Level capacities
- **Loop Integration**: Blocks may address your active core loops
- **XP Rewards**: +20-35 XP per completed block

### 2.2 Midday Presence (12pm-6pm)

#### For All Users
- **Presence Card**: Appears on Home tab when conditions are met
- **Visibility Conditions**:
  - Morning session complete
  - At least 1 block completed
  - Haven't done midday presence yet
- **Presence Overlay**: On tap, shows 3-4 AI-generated journal prompts
- **XP Reward**: +15-25 XP on completion

#### Level-Specific Depth
- **Level 1-6**: Simple, concrete prompts (e.g., "What are you noticing in your body?")
- **Level 7-12**: More abstract, pattern-focused prompts
- **Level 13-18**: Deep inquiry, witness-oriented prompts
- **Level 19-21**: Minimal prompts, maximum space

---

## 3. Evening: Reflection & Integration (6pm-10pm)

### 3.1 Evening Session

#### AI-Powered Evening Reflection
- **AI Conversation**: Deep Guru-style conversation that reviews your full day with context
- **Level-Specific AI Questions**:
  - Level 1-3: "What patterns did you notice today?"
  - Level 4-6: "Where did you find the witness?"
  - Level 7-9: "What loop showed up? How did you meet it?"
  - Level 10-12: "Where was the gap between stimulus and response?"
  - Level 13-15: "What dissolved today? What remains?"
  - Level 16+: Deep non-dual inquiry
- **Guided Journaling**: Structured end-of-day review
- **Theme Integration**: Connects day's experiences to your journey
- **Pattern Recognition**: AI helps identify insights from your day
- **Capacity Updates**: Markers updated based on day's activities
- **Tomorrow Preview**: Hints at what tomorrow might bring

### 3.2 XP Rewards
- **Morning session**: +15 XP
- **Each block**: +20-35 XP
- **Midday presence**: +15-25 XP  
- **Evening session**: +20 XP
- **Dream journal**: +10 XP
- **Streak Multipliers**: 1.1x (3+ days) → 1.5x (30+ days)

---

## 4. Night: Rest & Renewal (10pm-5am)

### 4.1 Night Mode
- **Visibility**: 10pm-5am
- **Grounding Practices**: AI-personalized level-appropriate exercises for relaxation
- **Focus Areas**: Breath, body, sense, release practices
- **Gentle Guidance**: Helps prepare for restful sleep

### 4.2 Dream Journaling (5am-9am)
- **Prompt**: "Did you dream last night?" appears on Home tab
- **Quick Capture**: Simple interface to record dreams
- **XP Reward**: +10 XP on completion
- **Context Integration**: Dreams feed into next morning's AI session

## 6. Edge Cases & Special Scenarios

### 6.1 First-Time User
- Special AI onboarding greeting
- Adaptive setup with AI guidance
- Guided block explanation

### 6.2 Late Start (after 12pm)
- Morning session still available
- Compressed adaptive block list
- Midday presence immediately available after morning

### 6.3 Missed Day Return
- AI acknowledges absence without judgment
- Fresh start approach
- Streak may be affected (or freeze used)

### 6.4 Level-Up During Day
- Celebration popup
- Blocks continue as normal
- Next day reflects new level

### 6.5 Night-Time First Visit
- NightWelcomeCard shows
- Option to skip to tomorrow
- Minimal grounding exercises available

## Summary

ConsciousOS is designed to meet you where you are each day. The AI-powered Adaptive System creates a unique daily flow that:

1. **Starts with AI-guided intention and awareness** - Your morning check-in with AI sets the tone
2. **Engages you in meaningful practices throughout the day** - Adaptive blocks personalized to your state
3. **Provides opportunities for reflection and integration** - AI-powered evening conversations

Your journey is unique, and the AI-powered Adaptive System adapts to your needs in real-time, helping you develop the capacities for conscious living at your own pace.
