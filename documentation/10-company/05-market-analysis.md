## Industry Trends

### 1. Mental Health Crisis Acceleration

- **1 in 4** people globally experience mental health issues
- **76%** of people in some countries lack access to mental health care
- Mental health disorders cost the global economy **$1T annually** in lost productivity

### 2. Digital Therapeutics Emergence

- FDA approving digital therapeutics for mental health
- Insurance beginning to reimburse digital mental health solutions
- Clinical validation becoming standard expectation

### 3. Corporate Wellness Evolution

- **73%** of employers now offer wellness programs
- Focus shifting from physical to mental wellness
- ROI expectations: $3 saved for every $1 spent

### 4. Consciousness Technology Maturation

- Neuroscience validation of meditation benefits
- Wearable sensors enabling biofeedback
- AI making personalization feasible

### 5. Generative AI Acceleration

- AI enabling hyper-personalization at scale
- Natural language interfaces for complex systems
- Reduced development costs for AI products
