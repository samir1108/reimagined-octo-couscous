## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: Product-Led Growth (Year 1)

**Focus**: User acquisition through product experience

**Tactics**:
1. Free tier with compelling AI features
2. Viral sharing mechanics (progress journeys)
3. Social proof notifications
4. Community challenges
5. Referral program with premium bonuses

**Target**: 100,000 registered users
**Conversion to Paid**: 5%

### Phase 2: Content & Community (Year 2)

**Focus**: Building authority and trust

**Tactics**:
1. Podcast and YouTube series
2. Free masterclasses and workshops
3. Teacher and influencer partnerships
4. Community events and retreats
5. Published research and whitepapers

**Target**: 500,000 registered users
**Conversion to Paid**: 8%

### Phase 3: Enterprise Expansion (Year 3-5)

**Focus**: High-value B2B contracts

**Tactics**:
1. Dedicated sales team
2. Enterprise pilot programs
3. Health system partnerships
4. Insurance reimbursement programs
5. White-label licensing

**Target**: $50M+ enterprise ARR
**Enterprise ACV**: $100K average
