## Phase 1: Product-Led Growth (Current → Year 1)

### Strategy

Acquire users through product experience rather than paid advertising, leveraging our AI personalization as the primary differentiator.

### Key Tactics

#### 1. Free Tier as Acquisition Engine

- **Generous free tier**: 50 practices, basic capacity tracking, community access
- **AI preview**: Free users get 3 AI-personalized practices per week
- **Viral mechanics**: Shareable journey summaries, achievement badges
