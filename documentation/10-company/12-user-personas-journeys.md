## Conclusions

### What Works Across All Personas

1. **AI Personalization** - Every persona highlighted AI adaptation as key value
2. **Capacity Tracking** - Visible progress drives continued engagement
3. **Loop Work** - Deep transformation feature resonating universally
4. **Community Features** - Social proof accelerates word-of-mouth
5. **Level Progression** - Clear structure provides motivation

### Key Success Factors

| Factor | Impact on Retention |
|--------|---------------------|
| Day 1-7 onboarding experience | High (first-week retention critical) |
| Day 14 capacity improvement | High (validation moment) |
| Day 21 habit formation | Critical (after this, churn drops) |
| Day 30 level advancement | Transformational (advocates created) |

### Recommendations

1. **Invest in AI adaptation** - Personalization is the key differentiator
2. **Build Loop Work depth** - Most transformative feature across personas
3. **Create enterprise pathways** - High LTV potential
4. **Optimize referral mechanics** - Viral coefficient critical for PLG
5. **Develop capacity metrics** - Evidence-based progress tracking
