# ConsciousOS Vertical Level System (V-Levels)

## Not a ladder. A deepening relationship with reality.

---

## Overview

The V-Level system maps consciousness development across **8 Bands** (0–7), each with **3 sub-levels** (Entry → Integration → Mastery), totaling **24 levels**. Each band represents a fundamental shift in one's relationship with reality — not a measure of worth.

The AI assesses your band through conversation analysis, journal patterns, task responses, and behavioral signals — **not XP accumulation**.

---

## 🌑 BAND 0 — THE SLEEPER

**Tagline:** "Life runs me"

Life operates on autopilot — reactions, habits, dopamine loops. You don't yet know you're asleep.

| Sub-level | Name | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| 0.1 | Autopilot | Stress cycles dominate. Dopamine loops drive behavior. No self-observation. |
| 0.2 | First Friction | Wants better sleep, focus, health. Begins seeking stability. Still outward-facing. |
| 0.3 | Readiness | Self-improvement identity forms. "Becoming a better me" as project. |

**Product Language:** Energy • Clarity • Performance

## AI Assessment — How Bands Are Determined

V-Level is assessed by AI through converging evidence, **not XP accumulation**:

- **Language Analysis** — awareness markers in chat and journaling
- **Practice Quality** — depth and consistency of meditation/observation responses
- **Behavioral Patterns** — reaction vs. response patterns across the day
- **Loop Resolution** — how unconscious patterns are identified and transformed
- **Self-reported States** — internal experience descriptions

### Minimum Time Requirements

| Band | Min. Days | Evidence Required |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 0 | 5 | Engagement consistency |
| 1 | 10 | Seeking behavior, curiosity markers |
| 2 | 21 | Consistent witness reports in practice + daily life |
| 3 | 30 | Pattern identification, emotional processing evidence |
| 4 | 45 | Gap reports, conscious choice documentation |
| 5 | 60 | Internal authority markers, reduced external seeking |
| 6 | 90+ | Ego dissolution markers, flow state reports |
| 7 | 120+ | Continuous recognition, non-dual language |
