## Part XVII: The Samadhi States

### Section 17.1: The Hierarchy of Absorption

Samadhi is the state of complete absorption in the object of meditation, representing the culmination of raja yoga practice. The traditional taxonomy describes multiple states of samadhi.

**Samprajnata Samadhi (With Seed)**

Samprajnata samadhi involves absorption while retaining awareness of the object. It is divided into four stages:

**Savikalpa Samadhi (With Diversity)**

The practitioner remains absorbed but still perceives distinctions:

- **Savichara**: With deliberation, subtle effort remains
- **Nirvichara**: Without deliberation, effortless absorption
- **Sananda**: With bliss, pleasure in the experience
- **Sasmita**: With I-sense, self-awareness remains

**Nirvikalpa Samadhi (Without Diversity)**

All distinctions dissolve:

- Pure awareness of the object
- No sense of seer, seen, or seeing
- Complete merger with object
- Still supported by an object

**Asamprajnata Samadhi (Without Seed)**

Asamprajnata samadhi is objectless absorption, the state of pure consciousness:

- No object of meditation
- Only pure awareness remains
- All mental fluctuations cease
- The gunas resolve into their source

**Sahaja Samadhi (Natural State)**

Sahaja samadhi is the integration of samadhi into daily life:

- Continuous awareness of pure consciousness
- Not separate from normal functioning
- Natural, effortless presence
- Enlightenment fully embodied

**Samadhi Indicators**

The ConsciousOS system recognizes samadhi through:

- Extended breath suspension
- Loss of body awareness
- Timelessness perception
- Bliss sensations
- Unity with object of meditation
- Spontaneous devotion or surrender
- Post-samadhi integration periods
