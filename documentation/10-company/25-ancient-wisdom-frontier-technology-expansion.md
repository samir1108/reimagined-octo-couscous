### 1.1 Additional Yogic & Indian Sciences

#### Siddha Tradition (Tamil Nadu, South India)
The Siddha tradition represents one of India's oldest spiritual science systems, originating in Tamil Nadu thousands of years ago. Siddhas (accomplished beings) developed sophisticated technologies for bodily immortality (kaaya saamarpayam) through specialized breathing, meditation, and alchemical practices.

**ConsciousnessOS Integration:**
- 18 Siddha asanas (standing poses) for kundalini activation
- Thokar (skull-breathing) techniques for consciousness expansion

The Nath sampradaya, founded by Matsyendranath and Gorakhnath, represents the synthesis of tantric and hatha yoga into a comprehensive path of liberation. Their technologies include khechari mudra (tongue lock), amaroli (urine therapy), and sophisticated pranayama systems.

**ConsciousnessOS Integration:**
- Khechari mudra preparation protocols
- Kumbhaka (breath retention) advancement sequences

- Gorakshasana (Yogi squat) for base activation

#### Kashmir Shaivism (Trika Philosophy)
Kashmir Shaivism represents the highest philosophical development of non-dual Shaivism, originating in the Kashmir Valley. Its technologies for consciousness expansion include spanda (creative vibration) meditation, pratyabhijna (recognition), and the 112 techniques of the Vijñāna Bhairava.

**ConsciousnessOS Integration:**
