## WHAT NEVER TO SEND (Especially Pre-Traction)

- Fake numbers. Ever. One lie kills every future conversation.
- "We're the Uber of meditation."
- "We have a patented algorithm." (You don't, and no one cares pre-traction.)
- "Can I pick your brain?" (You're asking for money. Own it.)
- Attachments. Links only.
- More than 3 specific details about the product. (Curiosity gap sells.)
