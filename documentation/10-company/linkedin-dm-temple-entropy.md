| "I have an app, let's partner." | "Not asking for partnership in a DM" — disarms pressure |
| "Can we jump on a call?" | "10-minute call, no deck" — low friction, specific ask |
| No reference to their work | Shows you actually know what Entropy is and what it measures |
| Ends with ask | Ends with respect + they can say no with zero guilt |
