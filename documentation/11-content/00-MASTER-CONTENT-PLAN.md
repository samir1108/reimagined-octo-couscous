## Visual Language

- **Pixar/Disney quality**: Warm lighting, expressive eyes, textured environments
- **No floating icons**: No metaphor UI. Reality only. ConsciousOS mirrors life.
- **Color palettes by series**:
  - App Reveal: Deep indigo + warm gold
  - Loop Series: Amber + rust + warm shadows
  - Engineer's Journal: Cool blue-grey + monitor glow
  - Yogi's Perspective: Earth tones + sunrise orange
  - Level Ascent: Gradient from dark to light across 30 videos
  - Observation: Muted naturalistic + sudden clarity bursts
  - Shadow: Dark purple + teal + candlelight
  - Relationship Mirror: Warm rose + dual-lighting (cold/warm split)
  - Workplace: Fluorescent + screen glow + grey
  - Philosophy Drops: Minimal — near-abstract, spacious
- **Recurring motif**: Infinity symbol (∞) appears subtly at each video's end — never forced

---

## Sound Design

- No stock music. Ever.
- Room tone + ambient reality
- One emotional sound per scene (breath, click, notification, heartbeat)
- Voiceover: inner voice quality — neutral, almost ASMR, NOT inspirational speaker
- Every video ends: one warm resolved tone → fade to silence

---

## Posting Strategy

- **Cadence**: 1 per day (300 days = ~10 months of content)
- **Series rotation**: Don't post entire series sequentially. Rotate between series for variety.
- **Suggested rotation**: App Reveal → Loop → Engineer → Yogi → Level → Observation → Shadow → Relationship → Workplace → Philosophy → repeat
- **Hashtags**: #ConsciousOS #ThePattern #WakeUp #InnerOS #LoopWork #ConsciousTech #SelfInquiry
- **CTA style**: Never "download now." Always: "ConsciousOS. See the pattern." or "The journey inward."
