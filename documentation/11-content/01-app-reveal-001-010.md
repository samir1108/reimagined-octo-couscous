SCRIPT 001 — THE PATTERN
Series: The App Reveal
Duration: 30 seconds
Character(s): Arjun
═══════════════════════════════════════

🟡 SCENE 1 — MORNING (0–6s)
🎥 VIDEO
Pixar-style 26-year-old male software engineer sits on the edge of his bed.
Phone already in hand. Eyes open, but not awake.
Laptop on desk from last night. Unread notifications visible.
He scrolls once. Stops. Scrolls again.
No floating icons. No metaphors. Just reality.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
Neutral, almost factual
"You wake up.
And before you choose anything…
your day has already started."

🔊 SOUND
Room tone
Very faint phone vibration

🟡 SCENE 2 — WORKING (6–12s)
🎥 VIDEO
Same character at his desk. Code on screen. Cursor blinking.
Slack message arrives. Then another. Then a calendar reminder.
He doesn't react emotionally — he just switches.
Tab to tab. Task to task.
His face stays calm. That's the point.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"You don't feel stressed.
You just keep going."

🔊 SOUND
Keyboard taps
Soft notification clicks (not dramatic)

🟡 SCENE 3 — PATTERN (12–18s)
🎥 VIDEO
Close-up on his face.
Same micro-expression repeats:
Jaw tightens. Shoulders rise. Breath shortens.
We see this loop twice in quick succession.
Nothing exaggerated.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"Same reactions.
Same moments.
Different days."

🔊 SOUND
Low, almost inaudible pulse (matches breath)

🟡 SCENE 4 — THE LINE (18–24s)
🎥 VIDEO
He pauses — not dramatically. Just stops typing.
Looks at the screen. Then slightly away from it.
For the first time, he notices himself.
No glow. No effects. Just awareness.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
Very soft, direct
"You think you're choosing.
But most of the time…
you're repeating."

🔊 SOUND
Everything drops out
Single quiet breath

🟡 SCENE 5 — CONSCIOUSOS (24–30s)
🎥 VIDEO
Dark, minimal space. The character stands still. Calm, not transformed.
A subtle reflection appears — not a UI, not a screen. Just presence.
Text fades in: ConsciousOS

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"ConsciousOS.
Not here to fix you.
Just to show you the pattern."

🔊 SOUND
One warm, resolved tone
Fade to silence

SCRIPT 005 — THREE PILLARS
Series: The App Reveal
Duration: 30 seconds
Character(s): Arjun
═══════════════════════════════════════

🟡 SCENE 1 — OBSERVE (0–6s)
🎥 VIDEO
Arjun in a meeting. Someone criticizes his code.
We see his hands clench under the table. Jaw tightens.
But — camera pulls back — we see him watching his own reaction.
A subtle split: him IN the reaction, and him SEEING the reaction.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"Observe. साक्षी भाव.
Watch the reaction
without becoming it."

🔊 SOUND
Meeting room ambient. Muffled voices. His breath — foreground.

🟡 SCENE 2 — INQUIRE (6–12s)
🎥 VIDEO
Later. Arjun alone. The sting still there.
He opens ConsciousOS. The Guru asks:
"The anger at the criticism — to whom does it arise?"
He pauses. Not typing. Just… sitting with the question.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"Inquire. आत्म विचार.
Not 'how do I fix this.'
But 'who is the one hurt by this?'"

🔊 SOUND
Silence. Single keystroke that never completes.

🟡 SCENE 3 — RELEASE (12–18s)
🎥 VIDEO
Close-up on Arjun's face. Something shifts.
Not enlightenment. Not peace. Just… space.
The tightness in his jaw softens by a millimeter.
The grip on the identity of "good coder" loosens.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"Release. नेति नेति.
Not this. Not this.
I am not the one who needs to be right."

🔊 SOUND
One exhale — longer than the inhale

🟡 SCENE 4 — THE CYCLE (18–24s)
🎥 VIDEO
The three moments replay in quick sequence:
Observe (the clench) → Inquire (the question) → Release (the softening)
Then again, faster. Then again — until it becomes fluid.
Not a technique. A way of being.

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"Three pillars.
Not steps to follow.
A way of living."

🔊 SOUND
Rhythmic — like breathing: in, hold, out

🟡 SCENE 5 — CONSCIOUSOS (24–30s)
🎥 VIDEO
Sanskrit text appears alongside English:
साक्षी भाव — Observe
आत्म विचार — Inquire
नेति नेति — Release
Below: ConsciousOS ∞

🎙️ VOICEOVER
"ConsciousOS.
Built on three pillars
older than civilization."

🔊 SOUND
Three ascending tones. Silence.
