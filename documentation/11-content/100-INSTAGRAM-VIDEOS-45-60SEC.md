# ConsciousOS — 100 Instagram Videos (45-60 Seconds)

## Master Content Plan: "This Is My Story"

---

## Format Specification

- **Duration:** 45-60 seconds per video
- **Scene Length:** Each scene exactly 6 seconds
- **Total Scenes:** 8-10 scenes per video
- **Goal:** Make viewers feel "this is my story — this app is for me"

---

## The Psychology of Connection

Every video follows this structure:

1. **Scene 1 (0-6s):** Hook — universal human experience everyone knows
2. **Scene 2 (6-12s):** Specific detail that makes it personal
3. **Scenes 3-7:** Build the emotional story
4. **Scenes 8-9:** The turning point — a question, not an answer
5. **Scene 10:** The mirror — ConsciousOS shows the pattern

**Key Rules:**
- Use "you" constantly — direct address for intimacy
- Start universal, get specific
- Ask questions, never give answers
- Never say "you should" or "download now"
- Connect to childhood/origin when possible
- Make slightly uncomfortable — that's where recognition lives
