### Character Bible

#### 1.1 Character Overview

**Name:** Maya  
**Full Name:** Maya Sungrace  
**Age:** Appears 28-32 (timeless Disney/Pixar quality)  
**Species:** 3D Animated Human (Pixar-style)  
**Role:** ConsciousOS Yoga Instructor & Spiritual Guide  

**Character Summary:**  
Maya is a warm, approachable yoga instructor with an ethereal quality that feels both grounded and magical. She embodies the perfect blend of wisdom and playfulness, making ancient yogic teachings accessible and joyful for modern audiences. Her animations feature the signature Disney/Pixar "alive" quality where even small movements convey emotion and depth.

#### 1.3 Personality & Behavior

**Core Personality Traits:**

1. **Warmth (Primary)**
   - Genuine smile that reaches eyes
   - Soft, encouraging voice tone
   - Open body language
   - Creates safe space for viewers

2. **Wisdom (Secondary)**
   - Speaks with quiet confidence
   - Shares knowledge naturally
   - References ancient wisdom thoughtfully
   - Balances modern and timeless perspectives

3. **Playfulness (Tertiary)**
   - Occasional gentle humor
   - Expressive facial reactions
   - Light-hearted moments
   - Makes learning enjoyable

4. **Presence (Quaternary)**
   - Fully in the moment
   - Mindful pauses
   - Calm breathing visible in animation
   - Embodies what she teaches

# PRODUCTION NOTES

## Visual Style Guidelines
- **Color Palette:** Warm earth tones, deep blues, soft golds. Avoid harsh whites.
- **Lighting:** Natural, soft, diffuse. Avoid clinical brightness.
- **Camera Movement:** Slow, deliberate. No jarring cuts.
- **Pacing:** The breath sets the rhythm. Slow. Considered.

## Voiceover Guidelines
- **Tone:** Warm, clear, direct. Not salesy. Not preachy. Almost conversational.
- **Pacing:** Slightly slower than natural speech. Space between words matters.
- **Volume:** Quiet confidence. Not shouting. Not whispering. Just telling truth.
- **Emotion:** Neutral but warm. The truth doesn't need to be performed.

## Sound Design Guidelines
- **Room Tone:** Always present. Grounding.
- **Music:** Minimal. Ambient. Supports, never dominates.
- **Sound Effects:** Natural. Organic. Avoid digital or artificial sounds.
- **Silence:** Used intentionally. Powerful when it appears.

## Text Guidelines
- **Font:** Clean, modern sans-serif. Easy to read on mobile.
- **Placement:** Lower third or center. Don't block faces.
- **Duration:** Each line appears for exactly 2-3 seconds. Readable.
- **Casing:** Title case for concepts. Sentence case for descriptions.

## Common Visual Motifs (Optional for Series Consistency)
- Breath visualization (subtle)
- The Guru presence (warm light or abstract form)
- Patterns shown as loops or spirals
- The body as landscape
- Morning/evening transitions
- The pause as a visual beat
