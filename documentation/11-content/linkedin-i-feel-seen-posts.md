## Key Copywriting Principles Used

1. **Start with their reality** - not your vision
2. **Name their specific pain** - not generic problems
3. **Show you understand** - "It's not you"
4. **Paint the contrast** - what they have vs what they could have
5. **Make them feel hope** - this could actually work
6. **End with invitation** - not pressure
