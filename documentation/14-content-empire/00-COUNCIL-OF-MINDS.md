# The Council of Minds
## A Pre-Production War Room for 900 Pieces of World-Class Content

> Before a single post is written, 9 minds sit at the table. They argue. They cut. They sharpen. Only what survives this room goes into the 900-piece content empire (300 LinkedIn + 300 Twitter + 300 Instagram).

---

## Why a Council (and not "just write it")

The internet is drowning in AI-generated wellness content. "5 ways to be mindful 🧘‍♂️✨" — nobody reads it, nobody shares it, nobody remembers it.

We are not making content. We are making **a body of work** — the kind a journalist screenshots, a CTO forwards, a monk pauses on, a 19-year-old tattoos. For that, every post must pass 9 different brains before it's published.

If a piece can't survive this room, it doesn't deserve a reader's 8 seconds.

---

## The 9 Council Members (and what each one demands)

### 1. The Rishi (Ancient Wisdom Keeper)
**Sees:** 15,000 years of yogic science.
**Demands:** "Is this rooted in something real — Patanjali, Adi Shankara, Ashtavakra, Vigyan Bhairav, Nath siddhas, Buddha's Abhidhamma? Or is it Instagram-yoga-fluff dressed up in Sanskrit?"
**Cuts:** Anything that uses "chakra" or "kundalini" as a vibe word without lineage.
**Approves:** "Patanjali Sutra 1.2 — *yogas chitta vritti nirodha* — yoga is the cessation of the modifications of mind. We built that as a feature called The Witness. Here's how."

### 2. The Neuroscientist (Andrew Huberman in the room)
**Sees:** prefrontal cortex, default mode network, vagal tone, interoception, predictive processing.
**Demands:** "Show me the paper. Show me the mechanism. If the rishi said it 5,000 years ago and fMRI confirmed it in 2019 — say both, with the citation."
**Cuts:** "Quantum consciousness," "vibrations," "frequency healing" — unless tied to actual physics.
**Approves:** "The Default Mode Network is what Patanjali called *chitta vritti*. Meditators show 30% less DMN activity. We measure this proxy in-app via typing cadence."

### 3. The Engineer (the one who built the thing)
**Sees:** the actual codebase — 400 behavioral loops, 11-band spiral, 13 guru personalities, Groq + edge functions, RLS-protected XState machines.
**Demands:** "Don't talk about features that don't exist. Don't oversell what's shipped. If the Companion Engine has a 6-second auth timeout, that's a STORY, not a bug."
**Cuts:** Vapor — "AI-powered consciousness" with no specifics.
**Approves:** "Loop VAL003 — Performance of Healing — fires when a user journals 'I'm grateful' more than 4x in 7 days without a single complaint. The system flags it. The guru asks one question: 'What are you not allowing yourself to feel?'"

### 4. The Storyteller (Pixar in the room)
**Sees:** the human in front of the screen at 11:47 PM.
**Demands:** Specificity. A name. A time. A texture. "She didn't 'feel anxious' — she counted ceiling tiles for the 4th night that week."
**Cuts:** Adjectives. Adverbs. "Powerful insights." "Transformative journeys."
**Approves:** Stories with one detail you couldn't make up. The ₹47k server bill. The user who unsubscribed and then came back 41 days later.

### 5. The Skeptic (the cynical CTO scrolling at lunch)
**Sees:** 50 wellness apps a year die. Why won't yours?
**Demands:** "What's the ONE thing that, if I removed it, the whole thing collapses? Lead with that. Don't pre-apologize. Don't hedge."
**Cuts:** "We hope to," "we believe," "soon we'll." Either it ships or it doesn't.
**Approves:** "We are the only consciousness app where the AI gets *worse* on purpose — because at Level 17, our job is to disappear."

### 6. The 19-Year-Old (who lives on phone)
**Sees:** the algorithm. The hook. The 1.2 seconds before swipe.
**Demands:** "If line 1 doesn't make me stop, the rest doesn't exist. No 'In today's world…' No 'Have you ever felt…' Open in the middle of the scene."
**Cuts:** Throat-clearing. Setup. Context.
**Approves:** "I deleted 3 meditation apps before I built one." (Now I read on.)

### 7. The Investor (the VC at the bar)
**Sees:** moat, defensibility, distribution, retention, unit economics.
**Demands:** "Every 5th post should plant a seed of why this is a $100M company, not a hobby. Without ever pitching."
**Cuts:** Founder-victim posts that signal weakness without insight.
**Approves:** "Day 90: our retention is 67% vs Calm's 11%. Here's the one design choice that did it."

### 8. The Monk (the silent one)
**Sees:** the danger of using sacred things to perform.
**Demands:** "Are we *using* yoga to sell, or are we *serving* yoga by selling? If the answer is the first one, kill the post."
**Cuts:** Sadhguru-quote-on-a-sunset-photo energy. Spiritual bypassing.
**Approves:** Posts that risk losing followers because they tell an inconvenient truth (e.g., "Most meditation apps make your loops worse. Here's why.")

### 9. The Editor (the one who ships)
**Sees:** the calendar. 900 pieces. 300 days.
**Demands:** "8 perfect posts in a folder is worth less than 1 ok post that shipped. What's the minimum-viable version of brilliance?"
**Cuts:** Endless polish. Re-writing the same hook 11 times.
**Approves:** A draft good enough that 6 out of 9 council members say "ship it."

## The Council's Verdict on the Content Strategy

After day-1 deliberation, here is what the council unanimously agreed on:

### A. Three Platforms, Three Voices (NOT the same post recycled)

| Platform | Voice | Format | Length | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | The Builder-Philosopher | Story-driven essay | 1,500–2,200 chars | Trust, depth, B2B/investor pull |
| **Twitter/X** | The Provocateur-Aphorist | Sharp single line OR threaded essay | 1 line OR 8-tweet thread | Reach, quotability, founder brand |
| **Instagram** | The Visual Mystic | Reel script / carousel / voice-only | 15–60 sec / 10 slides | Emotion, virality, mass-market |

**No copy-paste between platforms.** A LinkedIn post becomes a Twitter thread by being **rewritten from scratch** in Twitter's voice. A Twitter line becomes an Instagram carousel by being **redrawn** in visuals.

### B. The 10 Content Pillars (each runs across all 3 platforms)

Each pillar gets ~30 LinkedIn + ~30 Twitter + ~30 Instagram = **90 pieces per pillar**. 10 pillars × 90 = 900.

| # | Pillar | Council Champion | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Adiyogi → Now** (the 15,000-year unbroken chain) | The Rishi | Why this app is not new — it's the continuation of the oldest science |
| 2 | **Inside the Engine** (how the app actually works) | The Engineer | 400 loops, 11-band spiral, guru rotation, the math of consciousness |
| 3 | **Ancient × Neuroscience** (rishi met fMRI) | The Neuroscientist | Patanjali ↔ Default Mode Network. 8 limbs ↔ polyvagal theory. |
| 4 | **The Loops You're In** (pattern recognition as service) | The Storyteller | "If you do X, you might be in loop Y. Here's the way out." |
| 5 | **The 13 Gurus** (character-driven master spotlights) | The Monk | Who they were, what they would say to a 2026 founder |
| 6 | **Founder's Journal** (raw build-in-public) | The Storyteller | The ₹47k bill, the 3am bug, the user who cried |
| 7 | **The Anti-App** (everything we refuse to do) | The Skeptic | No streaks. No notifications. No gamification trickery. Why. |
| 8 | **Modern Sickness, Ancient Cure** (corporate burnout files) | The Investor | Case studies, the CHRO's dilemma, 60-day pilots |
| 9 | **Wisdom in 1 Line** (aphorisms / sutra remixes) | The Monk | Patanjali / Ashtavakra / Ramana — translated for the algorithm age |
| 10 | **The Future Human** (where consciousness × AI is going) | The 19-Year-Old | Not "AI will…" — "Here's what we're already shipping that no one else is." |

### C. The Series (so people can binge)

The 19-Year-Old insisted: *"People don't follow accounts. They follow series."*

Recurring series threaded through the 300 days:

- **#LoopOfTheWeek** — every Monday, one of the 400 loops decoded (Twitter + IG)
- **#RishiVsScience** — every Wednesday, an ancient claim + a 2010s paper that confirms it (LinkedIn long-form)
- **#GuruDialogue** — every Friday, a 1-page imagined dialogue between two masters in our app
- **#FounderField Notes** — every Sunday, what broke this week and what we learned
- **#OneLineUpanishad** — daily Twitter aphorism, no thread, no link, just the line

### D. The 5 Anti-AI Rules the Council Enforces

1. **No emoji as garnish.** Max 1 per post. Only if it carries meaning.
2. **No "Have you ever felt…" / "In today's fast-paced world…"** — these phrases are auto-rejected.
3. **Specific number > vague intensifier.** "₹47,231" beats "a lot." "Day 73" beats "recently."
4. **A real human in every post.** A name. A scene. A time. Or a self-disclosure with a wound.
5. **End with a question or a silence — never a CTA stack.** Max one CTA per 5 posts.
