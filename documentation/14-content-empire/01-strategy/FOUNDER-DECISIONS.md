## D1. Voice of "you"
**Decision: Mixed, weighted 70/30.**

- **70% second-person, direct.** "You open the app at 11:47 PM. The guru does not greet you. He waits. That silence is a feature."
- **30% third-person stories.** Real or composite users with a single-letter alias ("M, day 41…"). Used when we need distance — for posts about pain, failure, dark night, regression.
- Never first-person plural ("we believe…"). It signals committee. Either I speak, or the user speaks.

**Why:** "You" creates the mirror. Stories give the mirror a frame. Both are needed. Neither alone works.

---

## D2. Founder face
**Decision: Founder-led on LinkedIn + Twitter. Brand-led on Instagram.**

- **LinkedIn**: every post is signed `— Vatsal` (placeholder; replace with real name). Real photo on profile. The story IS the founder building this thing in public.
- **Twitter**: same — founder account is the primary, brand handle is secondary/repost-only.
- **Instagram**: brand-anonymous, visual-first. The product, the gurus, the loops are the characters. The founder appears 1 in 10 posts max — when the story demands it (e.g., "this is the bill that almost killed us" with hand holding a printed invoice).

**Why:** LinkedIn rewards humans, not logos. Instagram rewards aesthetics — a face dilutes the cinematic Temple universe.

## D3. Brand name in copy
**Decision: Use "ConsciousOS" sparingly. Use "the app" or "the system" 80% of the time.**
- Mention the brand name max 1× in any post.
- Never lead with the brand. Lead with the human, the loop, the rishi, the science.
- Profile bio + final line carries the brand. The body carries the work.

---

## D4. Citations & sources
**Decision: Every neuroscience claim cites a paper or it doesn't ship.** Bibliography lives in `01-strategy/source-bibliography.md`. If a claim has no source, rewrite it as a personal observation, not a fact.
