## Post 022 — "The validation loop"

Here's how it sounds: you do something. Then, before the moment is even over, your mind is already constructing how you'll mention it later. To your partner. To your team. To LinkedIn. To no one specifically — just to the audience that lives in your head.

## Post 026 — "The comparison loop"
