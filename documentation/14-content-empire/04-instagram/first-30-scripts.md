# Instagram — First 30 Scripts

> **Format mix:** 10 reels (visual-first, 30-45s), 10 carousels (text-first, 7-10 slides), 10 voice-only (audio-first, 60-90s, hook + one teaching, calm visual).

> **Posting cadence:** 1 piece/day, 7 days/week. Rotate format. Save best-performing format insights weekly.

> **Universal rule:** First 1.5 seconds = the hook. If they don't stop scrolling, nothing else matters. Hook then deliver — never the reverse.
