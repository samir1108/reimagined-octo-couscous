# Pillar 2 — Inside the Engine
## 30 Instagram pieces • Brand-anonymous, visual-first
## Mix: 12 Reels (15–60s) + 12 Carousels (10 slides) + 6 Voice-only Reels (15–45s)

> Instagram rule: the first 1.2 seconds is everything. If a thumb keeps swiping, you don't exist. Every piece below opens MID-SCENE, never with setup.

> Format key: **HOOK** = first frame/line. **VISUAL** = what's on screen. **CAPTION** = under-post text. Keep captions <300 chars; let the visual do the work.
