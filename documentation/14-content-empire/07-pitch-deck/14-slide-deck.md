# Investor Pitch Deck — 14 Slides

> **How to use:** Build this in Pitch.com or Figma. Each slide = one idea. No slide more than 6 words on screen. Speaker notes are what you actually say. Total deck read time: 4 minutes. Total pitch with discussion: 30 minutes.

> **Deck philosophy:** The deck is not the pitch. The conversation is the pitch. The deck is what they reread on Sunday after meeting you.

## After the deck — what to send

**Within 2 hours of meeting (always):**
- One-line thank you, one specific thing you remember they said, one resource that helps them (a book, an article, a person to introduce them to). NO ask in this email.

**Within 24 hours:**
- The deck PDF
- The unit economics spreadsheet (one tab, transparent)
- A 60-second Loom of you walking through Slide 5 in case a partner missed it
- A link to your LinkedIn (so the build-in-public is visible)

**Never send:**
- 40-slide appendix decks
- "Here's our roadmap" Gantt charts
- Anything with stock photography
- Forecasts that hockey-stick in Year 3 with no Year 1 grounding
