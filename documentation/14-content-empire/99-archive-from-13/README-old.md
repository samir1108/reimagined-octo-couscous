## How to use this book

1. Every day has a **Daily Card** in `00-roadmap/` (Day 001 → Day 300).

3. If you skip a day, don't catch up. Just start tomorrow's card.
4. All content is pre-written. You only edit names/numbers/today's feeling.

## The Non-Negotiables

1. **Post on LinkedIn every single day for 300 days.** No exceptions. The pre-written posts are in `01-linkedin/`. If you have nothing to say, post the scheduled one. If something happened today, write your own — but ship.

2. **Reply to every comment within 4 hours.** Algorithm + community both reward this. Set 3 windows: 9am, 1pm, 7pm.

3. **DM 5 new people every day.** Not pitches. Real conversations. (Playbook in `04-user-acquisition/dm-strategy.md`.)

4. **Send 1 cold email every day.** Govt, foundation, corporate, investor, press — rotate. Templates in `03-emails/`.

5. **Ship 1 Instagram piece every day** (reel OR carousel OR voice-only). 100 scripts pre-written.

That's it. 5 actions. ~90 minutes a day. For 300 days. You will own the category.

- 100 IG pieces × 1 viral every 30 = 10 viral moments × 100K reach = 1M+ impressions
- 100 founding members × ₹999 = ₹99,900 cash
- 3 corporate pilots × 100 seats × ₹500/seat after pilot = ₹1,50,000 MRR

**Total realistic outcome by Day 300:** 50K-100K registered users, ₹5-15L MRR, 1 grant secured (₹25L-1Cr), 5-10 angels signed, ready for ₹3-5Cr seed round.

## The Anti-AI Rules (read every Monday)

1. **No emojis as decoration.** One per post max, only if it adds meaning.
2. **No "🚀 Excited to share..."** Ever. Burn this phrase.
3. **No "I'm thrilled / honored / humbled..."** Show, don't perform humility.
4. **No "Here are 5 lessons I learned..."** Tell ONE story with ONE insight.
5. **Specific > clever.** "₹47,000" beats "a lot of money." "Day 23" beats "recently."
6. **Vulnerable > polished.** "I cried in my car after that meeting" beats "challenges taught me."
7. **Long-form wins on LinkedIn.** 1500-2200 chars outperforms short.
8. **End with a question, not a hashtag dump.** Max 3 hashtags. Or zero.
9. **No stock photos.** Real photos of you, your screen, your team, your city, or no image.
10. **If it sounds like ChatGPT wrote it, delete and rewrite.**
