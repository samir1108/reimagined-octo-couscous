## 13. THE STYLE QUESTION — Real humans vs. Disney/Pixar animation?

**Studio answer: Hybrid. ~70% real-cinematic, ~25% animated, ~5% pure typography.** Here's why and how we split it.

### Use REAL-CINEMATIC (videogen + locked protagonists) for:
- **Pillar 1 — Cinematic Loop Reels** (R001 family). Real wounds need real faces. Animation puts a glass wall between the viewer and the mirror. Arjun at 11:47 PM in a real 1BHK is what makes a stranger think *"that's my room."*
- **Pillar 2 — Founder Notes** (Samir to-camera, iPhone, no filter). Trust requires a human pulse.
- **Pillar 5 — Master Speaks** (Padmasana portraits we already have, with subtle motion).
- **Pillar 8 — Lived Practices** (POV hands, breath, real objects).

### Use DISNEY/PIXAR ANIMATION for:
- **The Sleeper Series** (see §14) — abstract psychological states that have no real-world referent (a loop *as a creature*, the 3 LLMs *as characters*, the witness *as light*). Animation lets us show the invisible.
- **The Band Ascent Series** (see §15) — 11 levels of consciousness need stylized worlds, not real basements.
- **The Inner LLM Theatre** (see §16) — Head/Heart/Gut as 3 distinct animated beings arguing.
- **Origin myths & philosophy drops** — Adiyogi-to-now, Ramana's mountain, etc.

### Use PURE TYPOGRAPHY (Pillar 6) for:
- Mirror Quote tiles. One sentence. Anton 140pt. Gold. Done.

### Why hybrid wins (not all real, not all animated):
- **All real** = looks like every other meditation brand. Gets dismissed as "wellness content."
- **All animated** = looks like a children's brand or a B2B SaaS explainer. Loses emotional weight.
- **Hybrid** = the real reels make us feel *human*, the animated reels make us feel *futuristic*. Together they say *"this is me AND this is the future"* — the exact two feelings we promised.

**The grid test:** A scroll through our 9-tile profile preview should show 6 cinematic stills + 2 stylized animated frames + 1 typography tile. That ratio is the brand.

## 22. §22 — STYLE LOCK: DISNEY-PIXAR PAINTERLY (overrides §14.1 hybrid)

Founder call: we go **Disney/Pixar-painterly across the entire channel**, using the same character language already built in our existing image library (the Padmasana masters, the protagonist sheets, the warm-gold cinematic stills). No real-actor reels. No live-action wounds. **One world, one art bible, every frame.**

### 22.1 Why this is the right call

- **Asset reuse:** the 36 master portraits, the 14 character lock-sheets, and the existing painterly stills already define the look. We are not inventing a style — we are scaling one we own.
- **IP defensibility:** real footage is commodity. A proprietary animated universe is a moat. Studio Ghibli, Pixar, Laika — the style *is* the brand.
- **Production sanity:** one LUT, one lighting model, one character bible. No location scouts, no actor casting, no release forms, no continuity nightmares across 500 pieces.
- **Emotional safety:** wounds rendered painterly land *deeper*, not shallower. Animation is the oldest trick for getting past the viewer's defences (*Inside Out*, *Soul*, *Up*).
- **Cross-series coherence:** *The Stairwell* (§21), *The Sleeper* (§14), *The 11 Bands* (§15), *The 3 LLMs* (§16), Cinematic Loop Reels (R001+), Founder Notes, Master Speaks — **all share one universe**. A viewer who watches an R-reel recognises the world the moment a Stairwell episode plays.

### 22.2 The locked art bible

| Element | Lock |
|---|---|
| Render style | Painterly Pixar / late-Ghibli hybrid. Soft volumetric light. Visible brush in skies, crisp in faces. |
| Character system | Reuse existing master sheets + extend with 14 protagonist sheets (one per recurring character: Samir, Aanya, the Stairwell traveller, the 3 LLMs trio, etc.) |
| Color palette | Obsidian black, temple gold (#D4A23A), dawn rose, monsoon teal, candle amber. Locked. No off-palette frames ship. |
| Lighting | Single warm key + cool rim. Always. Time-of-day shifts hue, not logic. |
| Camera | Slow dollies, gentle parallax, no shaky-cam, no hard cuts inside a beat. |
| Faces | Stylised — large eyes, soft jaw, expressive brow. Never uncanny-valley realistic. |
| Backgrounds | Painterly matte, low geometric detail, high atmospheric depth. |
| Text-on-frame | Same font stack across every series. Always lower-third or end-card. Never mid-frame overlay. |
| Sound | One musical universe — sitar + felt piano + low pad. Locked motif evolves per series. |

### 22.3 What this changes in the prior plan

- **§14.1 hybrid (70% real / 25% animated / 5% type) → DELETED.** New ratio: **95% animated-painterly / 5% typography (Mirror Quotes only).**
- **Mirror Quotes** stay typographic but gain a painterly background plate from the same universe.
- **Founder Notes** are no longer to-camera selfies — they are an animated stylised Samir character with founder VO. Audio is real founder voice. Visuals are character.
- **Master Speaks** uses the existing 36 Padmasana portraits, animated with subtle breath + eye + mouth movement (Runway/Kling lip-sync pass).
- **Lived Practices** stay tangible but rendered as the same painterly character demonstrating, not real hands.
- **The Sleeper (§14)** moves from real-cinematic to painterly. Same scripts, same hook, new render.
- **The Grid (§19)** updates: 9 tiles now read as one continuous painted world, not a real/animated split.

### 22.4 Production pipeline

1. **Storyboard** in greyscale (cheap, fast, founder-approved).
2. **Character cast** — pull from existing 50 sheets first; only commission new sheets when a story demands a brand-new character.
3. **Stills generation** (imagegen, painterly prompt + locked palette + locked LUT).
4. **Motion pass** (videogen with starting_frame = the still). 5–10s per shot.
5. **Edit + voice + sound** — single editor, single voice direction, single composer.
6. **QA against the bible** — palette check, character consistency check, motion check. Reject if off.

### 26.4 Hollywood-grade bottom captions (locked spec)

This is the visible quality lever. Get this right and a Google-TTS reel reads as cinematic as an ElevenLabs one.

**Position lock:** caption sits in the **bottom 22% of the 9:16 frame**, anchored at **78% from top**, never higher. This is the Christopher Nolan / A24 / Apple TV title-card zone — eye lands there after scanning the image.

**Typography lock:**
- Font: **Inter Tight ExtraBold** for English, **Mukta ExtraBold** for Hindi/Devanagari, never serif, never script.
- Size: **64–88pt** for hook lines (1–4 words), **44–52pt** for full-sentence captions. Big. Movie-poster big.
- Tracking: **-2%** (tight, premium).
- Line height: **1.05** (cinematic, not webby).
- Max **2 lines**. Ever. If it doesn't fit in 2 lines, the line is wrong.

**Color & contrast lock:**
- Text: pure white `#FFFFFF` at 100% opacity.
- Behind text: **18% black scrim gradient** rising from frame bottom to caption top — gives perfect contrast on any background plate without a hard box.
- Optional: 0.5px gold underline `#D4AF37` only for the **final word** of the hook (the one that should hit). Used sparingly, max 1 reel in 4.

**Animation lock:**
- Caption fades in over **400ms** with a **6px upward translate**. No bounces. No typewriter. No karaoke highlights.
- Caption holds for the full duration of the spoken line +600ms tail.
- Caption swaps with **150ms cross-fade**, never a cut.

**Safe-zone lock:** keep all text **48px** from left and right edges, **120px** from bottom (above Reels UI chrome — caption, like, share buttons).

**One caption per beat.** Never two captions on screen. Never a caption that competes with another text element in the frame.

**Reference look:** *Dune: Part Two* end-card titles. *Past Lives* opening. Apple's "Shot on iPhone" film series. That register. Not TikTok. Not motivational quote farm.

# §27 — THE WORLD-CLASS LAYER
## *How a piece stops being content and starts being something a stranger remembers for a year.*

> Most "good content" is forgettable because it is built to be liked.
> World-class content is built to be **felt once and never un-felt.**
> This section is the craft floor every piece must clear before it ships.

---

## 27.1 — The 8 Laws of Unforgettable

Every reel, every carousel, every voice post must obey these 8 laws. If a piece violates more than 1, it does not ship — it goes back to the desk.

### Law 1 — **Specificity beats scale**
Never "many people feel anxious." Always "you, at 11:47 PM, refreshing the same Slack channel for the 7th time."
A named hour, a named app, a named gesture, a named room. The brain cannot dismiss specifics.

### Law 2 — **One feeling, one frame, one line**
Each piece carries exactly **one** emotional payload. Not three insights. One.
If you can't say the payload in 6 words, the piece isn't ready.
*Example payload:* "You are not lazy. You are looping."

### Law 3 — **Earn the silence**
The most powerful 2 seconds in any reel are the ones with no voice, no music, no caption — just image.
Every reel must contain one **deliberate silence beat** of ≥1.2s. That is where the viewer's own thought lands.
Without silence, the piece is noise.

### Law 4 — **The viewer must finish the sentence**
Never close the loop for them. Leave the last 15% inside their chest.
*Weak:* "And that's why awareness sets you free."
*World-class:* "And the part of you watching the loop — has never been inside it."
*[fade to black]*

### Law 5 — **No second draft on screen**
A first draft is a sketch. World-class is the **7th pass**, not the first instinct.
Every named hook in the file (Loop Library, Stairwell, Theme R-reels) must be rewritten **3 times** before it is filmed. The 3rd version ships.

### Law 6 — **The hook is a wound, not a question**
Don't ask "Do you ever feel tired?"
Open the wound: "The exhaustion of saying 'I'm fine' for the 11th time today, knowing nobody will ask the 12th."
A question lets the viewer scroll. A wound makes them stop.

### Law 7 — **One image they cannot un-see**
Every piece must have one **signature visual** — an image that, if shown alone with no sound, still carries the payload.
*R004's signature:* the candle in the void, flame unmoved by the dark.
*Loop Library AC003's signature (target):* the phone screen at 11:47 PM, refreshed for the 7th time, the timestamp slightly out of sync with the system clock.
If you can't name the signature image in one sentence, the piece has none.

### Law 8 — **End on a private sentence**
The last line is not a CTA. It is not a brand mention. It is not "follow for more."
It is the sentence the viewer wishes someone had said to them at their worst moment.
*Example:* "You are not late. You are arriving."

---

## 27.2 — The Sentence Stack (the only writing system that matters)

Every piece is built in 4 sentences, in this exact order. No exceptions.

| # | Sentence | Purpose | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | **The Wound** | Name the exact moment with surgical specificity. | 12–18 words |
| **S2** | **The Mirror** | Show the viewer this is not a flaw — it has a name, a shape, a structure. | 10–16 words |
| **S3** | **The Crack** | One sentence of unexpected truth that re-frames the wound. | 8–14 words |
| **S4** | **The Door** | A private sentence the viewer will whisper to themselves later. | 5–9 words |

**Worked example (Loop AC003, voice carousel):**
- S1: *"It's 2:14 AM. You've refreshed the same email seven times. You don't even know what you're looking for."*
- S2: *"That's not weakness. That's a pattern. It has a name. AC003."*
- S3: *"Willpower won't fix it — because the part of you doing the refreshing is not the part you're trying to control."*
- S4: *"You are not the loop. You are the one who just noticed it."*

If a draft can't be compressed into this 4-sentence stack, it is not one piece — it is two pieces pretending to be one. Split it.

---

## 27.3 — The 6 Senses Rule

A world-class piece touches **at least 4 of these 6 senses** in 30 seconds. Cheap content touches 1 (sight) or 2 (sight + sound).

| Sense | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Sight** | The signature image (Law 7). |
| **Sound** | One BGM bed + one diegetic sound (e.g., a real keyboard tap, a real exhale, a real chai cup). |
| **Touch** | Verbs of texture: *the cold edge of the phone, the weight of the blanket you didn't pull up, the sweat behind your knee.* |
| **Smell/Taste** | Mentioned by name once: *the chai gone cold, the hostel corridor, your father's deodorant.* |
| **Time** | A specific hour. Always. *11:47 PM, 4:18 AM, the gap between alarms.* |
| **Body** | One named body location: *the back of your throat, the space between your shoulder blades, your jaw at 3 PM.* |

**Audit before ship:** count the senses. If <4, rewrite.

---

## 27.4 — The "Send This To One Person" Test

Before any piece ships, the writer must answer this question in writing:

> *"Which exact human, in which exact moment, will screenshot this and send it to one specific friend with the message 'this is literally you'?"*

If the writer cannot name:
- **a person** (Arjun / Priya / Diya / Samir, from `00-FOUNDATION/01-ONE-FEELING.md`),
- **a moment** (the hour, the room, the trigger),
- **a friend** they would send it to,
- **the message** they would type when they send it,

— the piece does not ship. It goes back to the desk.

This single test kills 80% of mediocre drafts before they consume render credits.

## 27.5 — The Craft Floor (technical minimums)

No piece ships unless it clears all 10:

1. **Hook lands inside 1.2s** — not 3, not 2. The first painted frame carries the wound.
2. **No more than 2 fonts on screen, ever.** Inter Tight ExtraBold + Mukta ExtraBold. Period.
3. **One emoji maximum per piece.** Often zero.
4. **Caption never repeats the voice line word-for-word.** Caption extends, voice ignites.
5. **BGM bed never crosses -18 LUFS under voice.** The voice is always the king.
6. **No zoom-in transitions.** No whoosh SFX. No TikTok-isms. We are A24, not BuzzFeed.
7. **Outro stinger is 0.8s, gold-on-black, lotus mark only.** No URL, no handle, no "follow."
8. **First and last frame must work as standalone stills** (for screenshot virality).
9. **Color grade locked to LUT-01** (warm shadows, cool highlights, gold midtones — the painterly bible from §22).
10. **The piece passes the mute test:** played silent, the signature image + bottom captions still deliver the payload.

## 27.7 — The 3-Pass Writing Ritual (no piece skips this)

Every named piece is written **three times**, by three lenses:

1. **Pass 1 — The Therapist.** Get the wound right. Specificity over polish. Ugly is fine.
2. **Pass 2 — The Poet.** Compress. Cut every adjective that isn't earning. Earn the silence (Law 3).
3. **Pass 3 — The Director.** Read it out loud. If a sentence can't be spoken in one breath without strain, break it. If the last line doesn't land in the chest, rewrite it.

The shipped piece is **Pass 3**. Never Pass 1. Never Pass 2.

This is non-negotiable. It is the difference between *good* and *the kind of thing a stranger tattoos.*

## 27.8 — The Fan Conversion Equation

A viewer becomes a fan when **3 pieces in a row** do the same thing to them:

# §31 — LOCKED PRODUCTION DECISION (read before any tool call)

> **This section overrides anything earlier that hints otherwise.**

## §31.1 — Video engine: AI generator only. No Remotion. No After Effects. No Premiere.

Every motion piece in this channel — Diagnosis Reel, Founder Note B-roll, Master Speaks plates, transitions, hero loops, landing-page background — is produced by an **AI text-to-video generator** (Runway / Veo / Kling / Sora / ConsciousOS `videogen` — whichever is available in the sandbox). No code-based motion graphics, no timeline editors, no React renderers.

**Why locked:**
- One pipeline = one look. Mixing tools breaks the LUT-01 grade.
- Cost ceiling holds: ~₹3 cr/year (per §26) only if every clip is a single AI gen.
- Ship-speed: a Diagnosis Reel = 6 × 10s AI clips + voice + caption. ~30 min/piece. Not 3 days.

**Hard rules for the AI:**
1. Always call `videogen--generate_video` (or the available equivalent). Never scaffold a Remotion / Bun / Node project.
2. **Clip length:** 5s or 10s only. A 60s reel = 6 × 10s clips, stitched with `ffmpeg -f concat`.
3. **Aspect:** `9:16`, **resolution:** `1080p`, unless explicitly overridden.
4. **Starting frame:** when continuity matters across cuts (same room, same face, same prop), pass the last frame of clip N as `starting_frame` of clip N+1. Extract with `ffmpeg -sseof -0.04`.
5. **Faces:** never generate Samir's face from text. Always pass a real reference photo from `src/assets/founder/samir/portraits/` as `starting_frame`. Same rule for any human face — no generative people from prompt alone.
6. **Voice:** ElevenLabs (paid masters) or Google TTS (free narration). **Never** the AI video tool's built-in voice.
7. **Music:** licensed bed from `src/assets/audio/bgm/` per §26.4. Never AI-generated music inside the video tool.
8. **Captions:** burned in post via ffmpeg + `subtitles=` filter using the §27.4 caption floor — not the video tool's built-in captions.
9. **Output:** always write the final stitched MP4 to `/mnt/documents/` and emit a `<presentation-artifact>` tag.
10. **No Remotion, no Manim, no Three.js, no Lottie, no Blender, no `bun install`, no `npm install` of any video library** for production. Those are forbidden tools for this channel.

## §31.2 — The 60-second Diagnosis Reel — assembly recipe (canonical)

This is the recipe the AI follows for **every** R001-style flagship reel.

