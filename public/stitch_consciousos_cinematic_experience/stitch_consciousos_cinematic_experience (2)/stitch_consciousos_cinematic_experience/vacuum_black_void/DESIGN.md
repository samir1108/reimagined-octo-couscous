## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, contemplative environment rooted in the concept of the infinite void. The experience rejects conventional flat black interfaces in favor of deep atmospheric density, evoking silence, deliberate focus, and modern ritual. It is crafted for introspective digital experiences, advanced consciousness interfaces, sacred data visualization, and ultra-high-end bespoke software.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Sacred Geometry and Subtle Luminescence**:
- **Atmospheric Void:** The canvas feels like deep outer space or quiet architecture at night—vast, calm, and deliberate.
- **Antique Gold Illumination:** Light is not used as flat utility, but as a warm, sacred filament piercing through vacuum space.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke tactile literary artifacts, disciplined by razor-sharp geometric sans metadata.
- **Architectural Discipline:** Grids, hairline borders, and strict spatial rhythms evoke monolithic monuments and temple proportions.

## Colors

The palette relies on absolute darkness balanced with warm ivory legibility and radiant metallic accents:

- **Base Surfaces:**
  - Canvas / Background: `#050505` (Deep Vacuum Black).
  - Surface Mid: `#090909` (Layered recess / secondary canvas).
  - Surface Elevated: `#101010` (Floating cards, modals, sheets).
  - Surface Highlight: `#181818` (Subtle hover states and interactive fills).

- **Typography & Glyphs:**
  - Primary Text: `#F1EDE4` (Warm Ivory) — luminous, dignified, and soft on the eyes in deep darkness.
  - Secondary / Muted: `#8D8981` (Stone Ochre) — subdued, balanced context that recedes effortlessly.
  - Quaternary / Faint: `#4A4742` (Deep Ash) — structure guides, inactive glyphs, and line numbers.

- **Accents & Illumination:**
  - Primary Accent: `#C5A46D` (Antique Gold) — key actions, active glyphs, focal selections, and sacred geometry nodes.
  - Radiant Accent: `#E8D3A3` (Luminous Champagne Gold) — hover gleams, subtle halos, key highlight micro-details.

- **Divider & Geometry Lines:**
  - Line Low: `rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.08)` — structural dividers, outer bounding lines.
  - Line Active / Gold: `rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.28)` — focused containment, active borders.

## Typography

The type system is built on a sharp duality between the contemplative, literary authority of **Playfair Display** and the crystalline precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Reserved strictly for displays, headlines, section introductions, and philosophical prompts. Headlines should embrace italicized flourishes for occasional focal words, evoking modern classical literature.
- **Technical Geometry (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Used for all body text, parameters, inputs, and systemic feedback. Keep body weights light (`300`) to retain a breathable, floating feel in the void.
- **The Tracking-Widest Archetype (`label-caps`):** Category tags, status pills, geometric coordinates, and navigational titles must always be set in uppercase with ultra-wide letter spacing (`0.22em`) to ground the interface with architectural order.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout in this system relies on high-volume negative space to instill clarity and deep stillness. Screen elements must never feel crowded; distance is a primary design material.

- **Column Framework:**
  - Desktop: 12-column layout with generous outer margins (`margin-lg: 5rem`) and `2.5rem` gutters.
  - Tablet: 8-column layout with `margin-md: 3rem` and `1.5rem` gutters.
  - Mobile: 4-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `1rem` gutters.
- **Rhythm & Proportions:**
  - Micro spaces (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) are used for inline metadata and tight icon-label groupings.
  - Component containers leverage `space-lg` and `space-xl` internally to prevent information density from eroding the void aesthetic.
  - Structural blocks decouple with `space-2xl` (80px), allowing contemplation between discrete modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids crude drop shadows. In an infinite void, light originates from delicate luminous objects or faint, atmospheric containment:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces rise from `#050505` to `#090909`, culminating in `#101010` for top-level interactive surfaces.
- **Ghost Hairlines:** Structural cards and panels separate themselves through razor-thin borders: `1px solid rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.08)`. Never use heavy, opaque solid outlines.
- **Sacred Gold Aura (Ambient Glow):** Key focal items (modals, active triggers, core geometry) cast a faint gold-tinted aura:
  - `box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.12), 0 20px 30px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9);`
- **Glassmorphism / Frost Layering:** Floating navigation bars and ephemeral drawers use `rgba(9, 9, 9, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an inner border `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometry is restrained, monolithic, and deliberate. While strictly sharp corners (`roundedness: 0`) can skew overly brutalist, a minimal radius (`roundedness: 1` / `0.25rem`) softens the contact points while preserving architectural discipline:

- Default elements (buttons, inputs, cards): `0.25rem` (4px).
- Structural containers and elevated sheets: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Sacred Geometry & Nodes: Circular elements (1:1 aspect ratio, `border-radius: 9999px`) appear exclusively for focal glyphs, cosmic rings, progress halos, and status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Sacred Action:**
  - Background: Solid `#C5A46D` (Antique Gold) or linear gradient (`135deg, #E8D3A3 0%, #C5A46D 100%`).
  - Text: `#050505`, set in `label-caps` (bold, tracked-out).
  - Hover: Subtle luminance pulse, box shadow `0 0 24px rgba(232, 211, 163, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Filament Action:**
  - Background: Transparent with `1px solid rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.12)`.
  - Text: `#F1EDE4` in `label-caps`.
  - Hover: Border switches to `rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.45)`, background shifts to `rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Monolithic Containers
- Background: `#101010`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.08)`.
- Padding: `2rem` (`space-xl`).
- Accentuation: Optional top hairline highlight using a gradient: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.4), transparent)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Canvas: Flat `#090909` with a subtle bottom hairline or 4-sided ghost border (`rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.08)`).
- Typography: Active values in `#F1EDE4` (`body-md`), labels float above in `label-caps` with `#8D8981`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.6)` with a warm 4px blur ambient glow. No harsh blue or raw white outlines.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Background: `rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.03)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.1)`.
- Text: `#C5A46D`, uppercase `label-caps` (`0.625rem`, letter spacing `0.25em`).
- Padding: `0.35rem 0.75rem`.

### Checkboxes & Binary Selectors
- Unchecked: Geometric diamond or rounded square with `1px solid rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.2)`.
- Checked: Core illuminated in `#C5A46D` with a centered geometric dot or micro-glyph; outer boundary softly glowing.

### Lists & Hierarchies
- Items separated by faint full-width hairline dividers (`rgba(241, 237, 228, 0.05)`).
- Trailing metadata displayed in `#8D8981` (`label-sm`).
- Hover shifts entire row to `#090909` with a gentle `#C5A46D` vertical accent tick on the left edge.
